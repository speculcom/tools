Vidu 是清华系生数科技的视频大模型入口：招牌是「参考生视频」多主体一致性—— 角色一致性要求高的叙事/动画向场景，它是国产阵营里最点这个题的。

Vidu is the Tsinghua-linked Shengshu Technology's video model entry: its signature is reference-to-video with multi-subject consistency — for narrative/animation work that needs character consistency, it's the one domestic player leaning hardest on that.

适合与不适合When to use it, when not to

适合：多角色叙事一致性、动漫风格动态化、国产直连使用。 不适合：模型自选、开放权重、写实大场面主打。

Fits: multi-character narrative consistency, anime-style motion, domestic direct access. Not a fit: model self-selection, open weights, photoreal big-scene work.
