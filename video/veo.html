Video generation for teams already in the Google cloud ecosystem wanting enterprise-grade data terms (paid = no training). The free-quota line is trained on and may be human-reviewed — self-check against that line.

Not a fit for an out-of-the-box creator studio UI — Veo itself is a model interface; the studio form lives in entry products like Flow.

权限与限制Permissions & limits

官方 Gemini API 附加条款（2026-03-23 生效）：非付费服务（AI Studio、免费配额）提交的内容与 生成回答会被用于「提供、改进与开发 Google 产品与机器学习技术」，且人工审核员可能阅读； 付费服务不用于改进产品，按数据处理附加条款处理。EEA/瑞士/英国用户即使免费配额也按付费口径。

适合什么任务Best for

已在 Google 云/Workspace 生态、需要企业级数据条款（付费不训练）的视频生成用户与团队。 免费配额线会被训练，别混；在意溯源标记（SynthID）的场景这条是加分项。

头号误解Top misconception

  • 把「付费不训练」套到免费配额——免费线内容与回答会被用于改进模型（EEA/瑞士/英国例外）Applying “paid means no training” to the free quota — free-line content and answers are used to improve the model (EEA/Switzerland/UK exceptions)
  • 把 Gemini Apps 隐私声明套到 API——两份文件、两套口径Applying the Gemini Apps privacy notice to the API — two documents, two sets of terms
  • 以为输出完全独占——官方明说不同用户可能得到相似输出Assuming outputs are exclusive — the official docs say different users may get similar outputs
