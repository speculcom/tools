数据标注：先定评测，再定标注格式Data annotation: fix the evaluation before the format

标注不是「把框画完」。先写清这条数据要喂给哪个评测或训练任务，再决定标什么、谁复核、争议怎么处理。图像、视频、3D 与文本的标注工具不同，但质量流程相同：指南、预标、抽检、仲裁与版本。Annotation is not “finish drawing the boxes”. First write which evaluation or training task the data feeds, then decide what to label, who reviews and how disagreements are resolved. Image, video, 3D and text use different tools, but the quality process is the same: guideline, pre-label, sampling, arbitration and versioning.

最小跑通顺序Minimum working order

  1. 先写 10 条标注指南与 10 个边界样例，再开始批量标Write a 10-item guideline and 10 boundary examples before batch labelling
  2. 用预标注 + 人工复核，抽检比例与仲裁规则先定好Use pre-labelling plus human review; fix the sampling rate and arbitration rules first
  3. 把数据集版本、标注指南版本与模型评测版本绑定Bind dataset version, guideline version and model evaluation version together

边界Boundary

站群没有做过标注工具间的吞吐或一致性对比；标注质量取决于指南、人员培训与仲裁，不取决于工具品牌。Prodigy 是闭源商业工具，价格为 2026-11 官方页：个人 $390 买断、公司 $490/席（5 席起），均不含税。涉及个人数据、生物特征或敏感内容时，授权、脱敏与存储期限要单独处理。The sites have not compared annotation tools on throughput or inter-annotator agreement; quality depends on the guideline, training and arbitration, not the brand. Prodigy is closed-source and commercial: as of November 2026 its official page lists $390 for a personal lifetime licence and $490 per seat in five-seat packs, excluding tax. For personal data, biometrics or sensitive content, authorisation, de-identification and retention need separate handling.
