Flowise 是「画布搭 LLM 应用」的开山一代（LangChain 组件族的可视化封装）：RAG/agent 流原型极快。 两个诚实标注：许可是 Apache 2.0 + enterprise 商业区混合；2026-08 后仓库推送放缓——热度与维护节奏要看快照。

Flowise is the founding generation of “build LLM apps on a canvas” (a visual wrapper over LangChain components): RAG/agent-flow prototyping is extremely fast. Two honest notes: the license is Apache 2.0 + an enterprise commercial zone; repo pushes slowed after 2026-08 — momentum is a snapshot fact.

适合与不适合When to use it, when not to

适合：LLM 应用（RAG、agent 流）快速原型、要自托管、要读源码。 不适合：要企业功能开箱（SSO 等在商业区）；要长期活跃发版承诺（按快照口径）。

A fit for: fast LLM-app prototyping (RAG, agent flows), self-hosting, reading the source. Not a fit for out-of-the-box enterprise features (SSO in the commercial zone); expecting a long active-release cadence (snapshot caveat).
