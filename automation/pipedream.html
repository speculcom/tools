Pipedream（Connect / Workflows）Pipedream（Connect / Workflows）

Pipedream
开发者自动化 云托管 代码优先 Connect 集成 partial lifecycle: active 核验Checked 2026-10-21

Pipedream 是「开发者向」的自动化平台：代码步骤（Node/Python/Bash）是一等公民，界面是搭流程的壳； Connect 线让「你的应用」成为别人工作流里的节点——被集成是它的商业主轴。

Pipedream is the developer-facing automation platform: code steps (Node/Python/Bash) are first-class citizens, the UI is a flow-building shell; the Connect line makes *your app* a node inside other people's workflows — being integrated is its commercial axis.

适合与不适合When to use it, when not to

适合：工程师做自动化、要代码级控制又想要托管运行时与 OAuth 托管；SaaS 要做集成面（Connect）。 不适合：完全不想碰代码（去 Zapier/Make）；要完整自托管（官方无此形态声明）。

A fit for: engineers automating, wanting code-level control plus a hosted runtime and managed OAuth; SaaS teams building an integration surface (Connect). Not a fit for teams that never touch code (go Zapier/Make); full self-hosting (no official statement of such a form).
