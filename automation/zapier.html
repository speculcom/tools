Zapier 是「连接器生态」的代名词——6000+ SaaS 集成的云托管自动化平台， 官方叙事已把 AI/agent（Canvas、Agents）提到主轴位置。没有自托管形态，一切在云端。

Zapier is the synonym for the connector ecosystem — a cloud-hosted automation platform with 6000+ SaaS integrations, whose official narrative now puts AI/agents (Canvas, Agents) at the main axis. No self-hosted form; everything runs in the cloud.

适合与不适合When to use it, when not to

适合：SaaS 间自动化是主轴、要把 AI agent 直接挂到现成连接器上、零部署。 不适合：数据不能出自有环境（去看 n8n 自托管）；要免费无上限跑量（看官方 pricing 页）。

A fit for: SaaS-to-SaaS automation as the main axis, hooking AI agents onto ready-made connectors, zero deployment. Not a fit for data that must stay in your own environment (see self-hosted n8n); free unlimited volume (see the official pricing page).
