n8n 是「自动化优先」的低代码工作流平台——AI 是它众多节点里的一类，不是它的身份。 它最容易被误读的地方是许可：源码公开，但它是 fair-code（Sustainable Use License），不是 OSI 开源。

n8n is an automation-first low-code workflow platform — AI is one node type among many, not its identity. The most misread part is the license: source is public, but it is fair-code (Sustainable Use License), not OSI open source.

适合与不适合When to use it, when not to

适合：跨系统自动化是主轴、要把 AI 嵌进既有流程、要自托管掌控数据与凭据。 不适合：把 n8n 包进产品卖给客户（商业协议线）；纯代码编排（去 OpenAI Agents SDK 等 framework 大类）； 开箱团队共享（Community 版工作流仅创建者可见）。

A fit for: cross-system automation as the main axis, embedding AI nodes into existing flows, self-hosting for data control. Not a fit for packaging n8n into your product and selling it to clients (requires a commercial agreement); pure code orchestration (go to the framework category); out-of-the-box multi-tenant SaaS (Community has no sharing).

模型与开放条件Model & openness

平台本身不带模型，AI 节点经各厂商凭据接外部 LLM（OpenAI、Anthropic、Google 等经 LangChain 节点族与专用节点）；具体节点覆盖清单未逐条核验，以官方 nodes 文档为准。 自托管时模型凭据在你自己的实例上。

The platform ships no model itself; AI nodes reach external LLMs via per-vendor credentials (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google etc. through the LangChain node family and dedicated nodes); the node coverage list has not been verified item by item — the official nodes docs are authoritative. When self-hosted, model credentials live on your own instance.

运行位置Where it runs

两条官方形态：自托管（Docker/npm，Community 版免费无执行上限）与 n8n Cloud 托管订阅。 自托管队列模式（worker 扩展）包含在 Community 内，multi-main 高可用属付费档 （官方 choose-n8n 口径，2026-10 第三方转述+官方 docs 对照）。

Two official forms: self-hosted (Docker/npm, free Community edition with no execution cap) and n8n Cloud managed subscription. Self-hosted queue mode (worker scaling) is included in Community; multi-main HA belongs to paid tiers (official choose-n8n framing, 2026-10 third-party paraphrase cross-checked against official docs).

本地文件Local files

自托管实例跑在你自己的机器/服务器上，工作流数据、凭据、执行日志都落在自有环境； 二进制数据外部存储（S3 等）属付费档。Cloud 形态数据在 n8n 云端。

A self-hosted instance runs on your own machine/server — workflow data, credentials and execution logs all land in your own environment; external storage for binary data (S3 etc.) is a paid tier. In the Cloud form, data sits in n8n's cloud.

关机后的任务Tasks after shutdown

工作流由实例执行：自托管实例常开即可承载定时与 webhook 触发；官方给 Cron/队列机制。 「电脑关了还能跑」取决于你把实例跑在哪——平台不替你保管关机后的执行。

Workflows are executed by the instance: a continuously running self-hosted instance carries scheduled and webhook triggers; the official docs provide Cron/queue mechanisms. "Keeps running when your laptop is off" depends on where you run the instance — the platform does not hold executions for you after shutdown.

权限与限制Permissions & limits

自托管权限边界=你的实例边界；Community 版工作流/凭据仅创建者与实例所有者可访问 （共享属付费档）。企业 SSO/RBAC 属 Business/Enterprise licence key 解锁。 许可边界比权限边界更关键：SUL 三条限制见 license 字段。

Self-hosted permission boundary = your instance boundary; in Community, workflows/credentials are accessible only to their creator and the instance owner (sharing is a paid tier). Enterprise SSO/RBAC unlocks via Business/Enterprise license keys. The license boundary matters more than the permission boundary: SUL's three restrictions see the license field.

许可是它最该先读的一页The license is the page to read first

官方 Community license 页给 SUL 三条限制（要点转述）：只能用于自有内部业务或非商业个人用途； 分发必须免费且非商业；不得移除许可与版权声明。文件名含 .ee. 的部分归 Enterprise License。

官方帮助中心进一步给了判例式口径：你在自己实例上替客户跑工作流、客户只拿结果——允许； 客户能进编辑器或经你的界面/API/MCP/AI agent 配置工作流——需要商业协议（n8n Embed）。

The official Community license page states SUL's three restrictions (key points paraphrased): use only for your own internal business or non-commercial personal purposes; redistribution must be free of charge and non-commercial; the license and copyright notices may not be removed. Files whose names contain .ee. fall under the Enterprise License.

The official help center adds case-law-style guidance: running workflows for a client on your own instance while the client only receives the results — allowed; the client entering the editor or configuring workflows through your UI/API/MCP/AI agent — requires a commercial agreement (n8n Embed).
