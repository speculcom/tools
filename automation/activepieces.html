Activepieces 是开源低代码自动化的「许可最干净」一档：主体 MIT Expat，只有 ee/ 目录是商业许可区—— 比 n8n 的 SUL 宽松，代价是连接器体量小得多。自托管 + 官方云两条线。

Activepieces is the cleanest-license tier of open-source low-code automation: MIT Expat for the main body, only the ee/ directory is the commercial-license zone — looser than n8n's SUL, at the cost of a much smaller connector catalog. Self-hosted + official cloud, two lines.

适合与不适合When to use it, when not to

适合：要 MIT 级许可自由度、要自托管、要能读源码魔改。 不适合：要海量现成连接器（先核你要的 piece 存不存在）；要企业功能开箱（SSO 在 ee 线）。

A fit for: MIT-level license freedom, self-hosting, reading and modifying the source. Not a fit for huge ready-made connector catalogs (check whether your piece exists first); out-of-the-box enterprise features (SSO sits on the ee line).
