Opal 是 Google Labs 的无代码 AI mini-app 搭建器：用自然语言描述，就能把生成、搜索、视频等步骤串成可分享的小应用。 官方已宣布 2026-11-17 从 Labs 毕业，工作流迁移到 Gemini skills；之后活跃工作流不再运行。

跨大类对照：LangGraph。

A no-code AI mini-app builder from Google Labs: describe it in natural language and chain generation, search and video steps into a shareable app. Google has announced the 2026-11-17 exit from Labs, with workflows moving to Gemini skills.

适合与不适合When to use it, when not to

适合：不写代码、想快速把「生成 + 搜索 + 视频」串成原型并分享的人。 不适合：要自托管、要模型自选、要长期稳定生产——官方已给出停运时间表。

Fits people who do not code and want to chain generation, search and video into a small app, or prototype and share quickly. Does not fit self-hosting, model choice, or long-term production (Google has announced the 2026-11-17 exit from Labs).
