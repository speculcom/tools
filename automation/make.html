Make（原 Integromat）是「流程图派」的可视化自动化平台——分支、迭代、路由器在画布上比表单派更直白； AI 是它众多模块之一，自动化才是身份。纯云托管，无自托管。

Make (formerly Integromat) is the flowchart school of visual automation — branches, iterations and routers read more directly on the canvas than the form school; AI is one module among many, automation is the identity. Pure cloud hosting, no self-hosting.

适合与不适合When to use it, when not to

适合：复杂分支/数据变换的可视化场景、跨 SaaS 自动化为主轴。 不适合：要自托管；要「AI 平台」定位（那是 Dify/Coze 的叙事）。

A fit for: visual scenarios with complex branches/data transforms, cross-SaaS automation as the main axis. Not a fit for self-hosting; wanting an “AI platform” positioning (that is the Dify/Coze narrative).
