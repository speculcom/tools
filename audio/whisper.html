Fits: privacy-sensitive transcription (audio stays local), offline environments, developer integration (MIT), multilingual. Not a fit: out-of-box meeting-product UX (it's a model), out-of-box diarization, heavy use without a GPU.

权限与限制Permissions & limits

Running locally means no account, no upload — privacy-sensitive audio (medical/legal/personal) going local is its irreplaceable scenario; the cloud API form follows OpenAI platform terms (kept at official category level).
