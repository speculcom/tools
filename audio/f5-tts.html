F5-TTS is an open-source flow-matching TTS model from Chinese academic teams (SJTU X-LANCE et al.) (MIT, ★15,487, last push 2026-10-23 on record): download the weights and run locally, Chinese + English zero-shot cloning is the lead use, no official hosted service.
