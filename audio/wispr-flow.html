Wispr Flow 是「按住说话、直接写进当前输入框」的听写层：核心不是转写文件，而是把口语实时变成可发送的文字；Canto-2 把专有人名和技术术语的识别率从 41% 提到 69%，同时没有明显增加“没说的词被插进去”的概率。

Wispr Flow is a dictation layer: hold a key, speak, and the text lands in the field you are already in. It is not a transcript-file tool; the point is turning speech into sendable text. The vendor says Canto-2 lifted proper-name and technical-term recognition from 41% to 69% without materially raising the rate of words you never said being inserted.

适合与不适合When to use it, when not to

适合：英文 / 多语听写、术语和人名多、写邮件 / 文档 / 代码注释；会议记录可接 Notetaker。 不适合：离线 / 气隙环境、要求本地模型、对会议音频留存极敏感、或需要中文方言高准确率的场景。

Fits English and multilingual dictation, heavy proper nouns and technical terms, writing email/docs/code comments, and meeting notes through Notetaker. Not a fit for offline or air-gapped work, teams requiring a local model, very sensitive meeting-audio retention, or Chinese-dialect accuracy as the main requirement.

模型与开放条件Model & openness

自研 Canto 系列；当前 Canto-2 为官方主推，称是 Canto-1 的 10 倍规模，用户不选模型。官方称覆盖 14 种语言，内部基准平均错误率从 9.9% 降到 6.3%，英语从 3.2% 降到 2.7%；这些是厂商自评，Canto-2 截至 2026-11-25 尚未正式进入 Hugging Face 公开榜。

In-house Canto line; Canto-2 is the current flagship, described by the vendor as 10× Canto-1 in scale, and users do not pick models. The official claim covers 14 languages, with average error rate down from 9.9% to 6.3% and English from 3.2% to 2.7%; these are vendor-run evaluations, and as of 2026-11-25 Canto-2 had not formally entered a public Hugging Face leaderboard.

运行位置Where it runs

Mac / Windows / iOS / Android 应用；听写在当前光标处直接输出。官方公开页没有说明推理在云端还是端侧，只说明服务已先在美洲上线、其他地区滚动开放；大陆官网可打开，但订阅与区域可用性未验证。

Mac / Windows / iOS / Android apps; dictation writes at the current cursor. The public pages do not say whether inference runs in the cloud or on-device, only that the service launched first in the Americas and is rolling out elsewhere; the mainland site opens, but subscription and regional availability were not verified.

本地文件Local files

不读取工作区文件；通过系统麦克风与辅助功能 / 输入权限把文字写进当前应用。Notetaker 可导入 Otter 转写，会议音频与转写存账户侧；导出文件归本地。

Does not read workspace files; uses the system microphone and accessibility / input permissions to write text into the active app. Notetaker can import Otter transcripts; meeting audio and transcripts sit on the account side, and exported files are local.

关机后的任务Tasks after shutdown

听写是前台实时交互，没有“关机后续跑”的队列。Notetaker 可录会议并在后台生成笔记 / 摘要，属于异步处理；官方未承诺离线或断网续传。

Dictation is a foreground, real-time interaction with no queue that keeps running after shutdown. Notetaker can record a meeting and generate notes / summaries in the background, which is asynchronous processing; the vendor does not promise offline or interrupted-network resume.

工具与扩展Tools & extensions

可在任意文本框里用，不绑定单一编辑器；有自定义词典、片段与 Notetaker。Notetaker 官方产品面给出日历 / Slack 接入和 MCP 导出到 Claude、ChatGPT 等工具；没有开放插件市场。

Works in any text field and is not tied to one editor; includes a custom dictionary, snippets and Notetaker. The official Notetaker surface lists calendar / Slack connections and MCP export into tools such as Claude and ChatGPT; there is no open plugin marketplace.

上下文与记忆Context & memory

个人词典是主要长期记忆，官方称词典累积到 1,000 条时仍能识别 92% 的较简单术语；Notetaker 可跨会议问答。官方未公开上下文窗口或记忆条数上限，不能按通用大模型上下文来估算。

The personal dictionary is the main long-term memory; the vendor says that at 1,000 entries it still recognises 92% of simpler terms. Notetaker supports cross-meeting Q&A. The vendor does not publish a context window or memory-item limit, so it cannot be estimated like a general-purpose model context.
