Udio is the other half of the music-generation duo (v1.5 Allegro line, complex-genre reputation), now mid-transition from copyright defendant to licensed platform — UMG/WMG have settled, the new platform goes walled-garden.

Fits: music creation experience (complex genres), tracking the licensed-platform new form. Not a fit: commercial pipelines that download and carry away (walled-garden direction), needing stable terms (transition period).
