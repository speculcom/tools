CosyVoice 是阿里语音团队（QwenAudio/原 FunAudioLLM）的开源 LLM-TTS 模型族（Apache-2.0， v1→v2→Fun-CosyVoice 3.0 三代线）：下载权重本机推理/训练/部署全栈，中文+方言覆盖与 zero-shot 克隆是主打—— 它是模型不是产品，官方不卖托管线。

CosyVoice is the Alibaba speech team's (QwenAudio, formerly FunAudioLLM) open-source LLM-TTS model family (Apache-2.0, three generations v1 → v2 → Fun-CosyVoice 3.0): download the weights and run inference/training/deployment locally — Chinese + dialect coverage and zero-shot cloning are the pitch; it is a model, not a product, and the official line sells no hosted service.

适合与不适合When to use it, when not to

适合：本机/私有部署中文+方言 TTS、zero-shot 克隆与微调全栈、Apache-2.0 商用底座。 不适合：要托管 API 开箱即用、要零运维。

Fits: local/private Chinese + dialect TTS, zero-shot cloning with a full training stack, an Apache-2.0 commercial base. Not a fit: out-of-box hosted APIs (the official line sells none), zero-ops setups.
