Permissions & limits

Desktop-app permissions follow the OS grant; no account system, and under the offline positioning no cloud-sync permission surface seen.

Best for

Fits: wanting an open-source desktop GUI for offline local models (vs closed-source LM Studio). Does not fit: service-style multi-model deployment; wanting latest models out of the box (the model-acquisition experience is weaker than Ollama's).
