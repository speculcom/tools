Model & openness

In-house Dream Machine video model line (official-site narrative); plus the Ray model line (seen on the official site). Users do not pick bare models. An open-weights line — outside the official product pages.

Where it runs

Web (lumalabs.ai) + API (an API entry on the official site, details kept at official category level); generation on the vendor's cloud. The company started in NeRF/3D capture (the original-Luma narrative); video generation became the main product line later.

Local files

Works stored account-side in the cloud, generation history visible on the web works page; downloads go local. No officially stated local data surface.

Tasks after shutdown

Generation is instant/queued (varies by tier, kept at official category level); no "keep-running-after-shutdown" task surface (outside the official product pages).

Tools & extensions

Text-to-video, image-to-video, keyframe interpolation (first/last frames, per the official narrative), Modify/extend editing lines (seen on the official site); the 3D/NeRF capture line is still a visible product surface on the official site (alongside the video line).

Context & memory

Organized via the account works page (generation history reviewable); cross-session memory — outside the official product pages. First/last-frame control is an in-task constraint, not cross-task memory.

Permissions & limits

Account + subscription system (D9: prices not collected); the training-use switch documented at official category level; free-tier commercial and watermark limits follow the terms (kept at official category level).

Best for
