When to use it, when not to

Creators who care about image-to-video quality and work in the Chinese ecosystem. Model & openness

Model family disclosed on the official release line: Kling 4.0 officially launched 2026-10-15 (confirmed by the Kuaishou investor-relations press release 2026-10-16: up to 30 seconds per native generation, universal reference up to 15 materials/10 keyframes, up to 10-bit HDR, 8000-token prompt input); Kling 4.0 Flash opened first for high-frequency scenarios (late-2026-09 closed-beta framing).

Where it runs

Three lines: web / app / API; generation processed in the cloud; plus a Team Space.

Local files

Supports image/video/subject/audio multimodal reference input (the 4.0 universal-reference framing is in the official press release); the processing stance for uploaded content is in the permissions axis.

Tasks after shutdown

Generation is task-based: submitted tasks queue in the cloud and outputs enter the account asset library; tasks run in the cloud independent of your machine being online, and there is no keep-running-after-shutdown product form.

Tools & extensions

The API line is public: the official Dev Pricing page lists models such as Kling 3.0 Turbo with per-second billing, and Kling Skills supports third-party agents (Claude Code/Cursor/Codex etc.); plans and quotas follow the console. In-platform community mechanics like Recreate/download (ToS §4.8); aggregated and called by third-party platforms (e.g. Runway MCP listed Kling 3.0/O3, see the Runway changelog 2026-09-18).

Context & memory

Generation is task-based, no cross-session conversation-memory form; creative continuity relies on the asset library and reference inputs (4.0's universal reference of up to 15 materials is an in-request capability, not session memory).

Permissions & limits

Official international ToS (effective 2026-04-21) §4.7.3(f): the platform may process content to "create, test, improve, train" its models; §4.7.4 opt-out goes through email support@kling.ai (revocation is prospective, not retroactive over completed training). §4.8: content published to the community can be Recreated/downloaded by other users.

Best for

People who weight image-to-video quality and the Chinese ecosystem. Commercial use must first pass the membership line (see the license column); publishing to the community equals going public; the training opt-out is an email request and prospective only. Developers who need direct API access go through the now-public Dev API line.

Licence and terms of use

Official international ToS §4.4: 'You own all intellectual property rights and other proprietary interests in and to the Content under applicable law… We do not claim ownership of the Content.' But §4.6: 'without our written permission, you may not use, reproduce, distribute… the Output for any commercial purposes' — commercial use requires written permission. The membership commercial license is governed by the Terms of Paid Service — the official terms entry is on record; clause details are left to the original text. Non-members must label output with Kling branding per §4.5.

When something fails

Account and permissions · Rate limits and retries · File-reading issues

Top misconception

  • Reading “ownership belongs to the user” as “commercialize freely” — §4.6 requires written permission for commercial use, opened by the membership tier
  • Treating the training opt-out as immediate — the email request is future-only and doesn't retroact on completed training
  • Mixing international and domestic terms — two product term sets; this profile's main axis is the international version
