8 questions, each comparable item by item with its peers.

Model & openness

Two forms: Runway's own Gen-series models + third-party model aggregation: the changelog shows the platform can call Seedance 2.5, Kling 3.0/O3, Ideogram 4.5, Seedream 5.0 Flash, ElevenLabs V4 and other third-party models (2026-09~10 entries, direct-fetched and verified 2026-10-21); tier routing follows the Free/Standard/Pro/Enterprise line (the pricing page is authoritative).

Where it runs

Mainly a web app, plus a DaVinci Resolve plugin (2026-09-23), Premiere Pro/After Effects plugins (2026-09-08 entry) and other in-host entries; generation processed on Runway's cloud.

Local files

Supports uploading materials (image/video/audio) as input; materials organized via the Brand Kits asset library; the training stance for uploaded content is in the permissions axis.

Tasks after shutdown

Generation is task-based; the Agent form can plan multi-step generation (Seedance Draft Mode entry 2026-10-02); no official statement of a "keep-running-after-shutdown" form.

Tools & extensions

Runway MCP is the official extension surface: Cursor Marketplace (2026-09-23), Grok Bot plugin (2026-09-17), OpenAI Dots integration (2026-10-01) all appear in the changelog; the platform also aggregates third-party generation models for calling.

Context & memory

Brand Kits provide brand-asset reuse (the Agent can create/edit them, 2026-09-10 entry); no cross-session conversation-memory form.

Permissions & limits

Official ToU §4.4: inputs and outputs may be used to train and improve its models — the terms state users grant a "non-exclusive, irrevocable, perpetual" training license; §4.2 the platform does not claim ownership of Your Content, but content in public areas grants broad licenses. No training opt-out switch seen.

Best for

Teams that weight video-generation workflows and want API/MCP integration. If you care about "materials never entering training" confirm first — at the clause level there is no opt-out; enterprise credit controls (Project Credit Caps 2026-10-09) are a team-facing plus.

Licence and terms of use

Official Terms of Use §4.4 'User Inputs and Outputs': 'The Company does not claim ownership of any of your Inputs or Outputs.' + 'Subject to your compliance with the Agreement, the Company does not restrict your commercial use of your Outputs.' §10.1(a) warns outputs may not be unique. §5(viii) bars using outputs to develop competing products. API embedding requires 'Powered by Runway' branding per guidelines.

When something fails

Account and permissions · Rate limits and retries · File-reading issues

Top misconception

  • Training-sensitive users assuming they can opt out — the terms grant an irrevocable perpetual training license with no switch
  • Reading “unrestricted commercial use” as unconditional — it is conditioned on complying with the terms, and §5(viii) bans building competing products
  • Treating third-party models on the platform (Kling/Seedance etc.) as one Runway terms set — generation comes from multiple providers; the platform agreement is the interface, but each model source has its own positioning
