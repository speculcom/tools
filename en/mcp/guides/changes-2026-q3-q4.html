Q3–Q4 2026 changes and migration notes

Planning

Ten version, entry-point and migration records, distinguishing event dates, effective/unconfirmed dates and post-shutdown migration paths, with implications for earlier choices.

How to use this page

Distinguish event/effective dates, source-page dates and this site's check date. These ten records are not a complete changelog for every product.

  • Check whether your version, region and subscription are affected.
  • A working link does not establish that all features, terms or migration steps were verified.
  • Mark unconfirmed launch dates rather than inferring them from current documentation.
  • For shutdowns, preserve needed data and inspect the destination's rebuilding requirements.

Effective shutdowns

Opal: shut down on 2026-11-17. The official FAQ states opal.google and Gems made by Labs were turned down the same day; existing workflows and shared Opal links no longer run. Opal files remain in My Drive > Opal, and prompts can be copied into Gemini skills or AI Studio for rebuilding.

Choose Gemini skills or AI Studio for the task and revalidate inputs, tools and outputs rather than assuming an equivalent copy.

Cursor's launch date remains unconfirmed; that is separate from the execution paths described in its current documentation.

Why ten records

Include changes affecting entry points, migration, permissions or version choices, with official sources. Distinguish confirmed dates from unknown dates. Related releases can share a record; the number of records does not establish completeness.

Verification scope

On 2026-11-04 the listed official pages were checked for the corresponding event, page date or release. Corrections cover the OneLogin page date, Claude's October 21 event, a later ExLlamaV3 release and Opal's lack of automatic workflow migration. On 2026-11-25 only Opal was rechecked: the official FAQ and opal.google show shutdown on 2026-11-17, Gems made by Labs closed the same day, shared links no longer run, and Drive files remain in My Drive > Opal. Other records retain the 11-04 scope.

Cursor's launch date was not confirmed. No installation, login, execution or migration test was performed, and other product-dossier fields were not all rechecked.

What to do next

  • Record constraints, acceptance and unknowns
  • Verify the result and its sources
  • Troubleshoot by symptom
