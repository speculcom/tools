8 questions, one table

8 questions, each comparable item by item with its peers.

Model & openness

The main line is Zhipu’s in-house GLM family, with a public model-release line on the Zhipu site; GLM open weights sit in the same family as cloud chat, while local execution belongs to the runner entries. In-app model tiers follow the logged-in UI, and the official privacy policy names Q&A, content generation and agent interaction as basic functions (2026-11-21).

Where it runs

Web (chatglm.cn) plus iOS/Android apps; generation runs in the vendor cloud and no model runs locally. The developer entry is the open platform (open.bigmodel.cn), a second interface from the same vendor; the plugin runs inside browser pages while inference stays in the cloud (2026-11-21).

Local files

Chat history and knowledge-base files live on the account. The official privacy policy says conversation records are kept to show history and deleted or anonymised after account cancellation, user deletion or the necessary period. Knowledge-base documents are parsed and retrieved in the cloud; no model runs locally, and downloaded outputs belong to the local machine (2026-11-21).

Tasks after shutdown

Chat responses return immediately; agents and plugins execute in the cloud, and the official specific-function rules say the plugin’s advanced mode requires screenshot authorisation and acting on the user’s behalf on sub-pages. Official pages offer no pause/resume task queue; long tasks should be measured per agent entry (2026-11-21).

Tools & extensions

The product includes data analysis (code-interpreter style), text-to-image, web retrieval and PPT generation, with agent creation and plugin entries. The official specific-function rules list plugin general/advanced modes and a skills marketplace; MCP support is not listed officially and requires wrapping the APIs yourself (2026-11-21).

Context & memory

Chat history syncs on login; the knowledge base is an explicit cross-session data surface, and the official privacy policy treats conversation records and knowledge bases separately. Finer-grained long-term memory and user profiles are not listed officially and should be checked in product settings (2026-11-21).

Permissions & limits

Accounts register by phone number. The official privacy policy says conversation records are kept to show history and deleted or anonymised after cancellation, deletion or the necessary period. Plugins need browser-page information and screenshot authorisation; training-use switches follow in-app privacy settings; the GLM filing number is in the cn block (2026-11-21).

Best for

Fit: Chinese long-document parsing and retrieval Q&A, the GLM open-weight family for cloud-plus-local choice, and student/workplace document work. Not fit: office/social-suite integration or fully leaving the account system (2026-11-21).

Licence and terms of use

Free with login. Official terms page (chatglm.cn/terms fetched 2026-10-22); output ownership and training terms follow those terms and the privacy policy (2026-11-21).

Top misconception
