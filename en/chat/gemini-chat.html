The general chat assistant inside the Google ecosystem: web, Windows and macOS covered, models split by subscription tier — “it won't train on your raw Gmail/Drive, but it does train on summaries produced while answering” is its most easily misread clause.

When to use it, when not to

People already working inside the Google ecosystem (Search, Workspace, Chrome) — the integration is a real advantage. If training matters, turn off Keep Activity (future conversations only); per the official reminder, don't enter confidential content.

Not a fit for running locally or freely swapping models — see the local model runners and open-source frameworks.

Model & openness

The model family is disclosed via official release notes and routed by subscription tier: Gemini 4 Argon rolled out globally to Google AI Pro & Ultra on 2026-10-13; Gemini 4 Carbon opened to Ultra and paying developers on 2026-10-28/11-10; Gemini 4 Neon entered the Gemini app and API on 2026-11-18. Free and paid tiers get different models — per the release notes.

Where it runs

Web is the main entry; official desktop apps exist — a Windows app (release notes 2026-09-10, Alt+Space to summon) and a macOS app (2026-07-29 voice-creation entry, 2026-10-13 Antigravity coding entry); generation is processed on Google's cloud.

Local files

Supports multimodal input and file uploads; the official privacy notice states Google does not train directly on your Gmail/Drive/Photos source libraries, but summaries, excerpts and inferences produced during answering are used for training (Connected Apps personalization page).

Tasks after shutdown

Chats return instantly; the Skills feature (release notes 2026-09-30) is a reusable instruction set with automatic matching/triggering, but there is no official statement of an "offline continuation" form.

Tools & extensions

Google ecosystem integration (Search, Workspace, Chrome) is the main selling point; Connected Apps can connect third-party tools (incl. MCP server tools, official help page 16836988); the Gemini macOS app embeds the Antigravity coding agent (2026-10-13).

Context & memory

Has memory and personalization; Gems (custom instructions) are being replaced by Skills and migrate automatically (release notes 2026-09-30). The full boundary of cross-session memory is per the official help pages.

Permissions & limits

Official Gemini Apps privacy notice (updated 2026-10-13): with Keep Activity on, prompts, generated content and feedback are used to improve Google services (incl. training generative models); with it off, future chats are not used for training (unless you submit feedback); temporary chats and activity-recording-off data are kept 72 hours and not used for training; human reviewers review some data — the official notice warns against entering confidential information.

Best for

For people already working inside the Google ecosystem — the integration is a real advantage. For training concerns, turn off Keep Activity; for confidential content, follow the official warning and don't enter it. For running models locally or swapping models freely, closed cloud assistants like this are not suitable.

Licence and terms of use

Official Gemini API Additional Terms, 'Use of Generated Content': Google does not claim ownership of generated content; commercial use must comply with applicable law, and API-returned content may require attribution to your users. Google notes outputs may be similar across users and reserves watermarking and SynthID provenance marks.

When something fails

Account and permissions · Rate limits and retries · File-reading issues

Top misconception

  • Reading "does not train directly on Gmail/Drive originals" as "your chats are not used for training" — summaries and inferences from answers are still used (official wording)
  • Confusing Keep Activity with temporary chat — two mechanisms: one governs future training, the other retention and history
  • Applying API terms to the consumer app — the Gemini Apps Privacy Notice and the Gemini API Additional Terms are two documents
  • Treating Deep Research as "one search" — it runs multi-step research on Gemini 3.1 Pro and since 2026-04-21 supports MCP and custom sources
