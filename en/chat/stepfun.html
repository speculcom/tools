8 questions, one table

8 questions, each comparable item by item with its peers.

Model & openness

StepFun’s self-developed Step family has an open-weight line on its Hugging Face organisation page (huggingface.co/stepfun-ai returned 200 on the day). In-product model choice follows the logged-in UI. The official privacy policy treats chat, voice, video calls and task execution as one product surface (2026-11-21).

Where it runs

Web (chat.stepfun.com, branded “JieYue AI”) plus apps; generation runs in StepFun’s cloud and no model runs locally. The page lists StepClaw and Step Audio Studio (ASR) entry points; open weights are a separate line requiring self-deployment (2026-11-21).

Local files

Chat history syncs on login; uploaded voice, video, images and documents are processed in StepFun’s cloud, and the official privacy summary lists microphone, camera and photo permissions with their scenarios. No model runs locally and no local data surface is listed; downloaded outputs belong to the local machine (2026-11-21).

Tasks after shutdown

Chat responses return immediately; StepClaw and other agents execute multi-step work in the cloud, and the official privacy policy lists “intelligent dialogue and task execution” as a collection scenario. Official pages provide no user-facing pause, resume or queue panel; long-task state should be checked at the specific entry (2026-11-21).

Tools & extensions

The page exposes Web Search Tool, knowledge-base Q&A, StepClaw and Step Audio Studio; the official privacy policy names intelligent dialogue and task execution as service scenarios. MCP support is not listed officially and requires wrapping the APIs yourself (2026-11-21).

Context & memory

Chat history syncs on login, and the official personal-information list names “historical conversation records” as data used for query and management. Conversation data can be used for training after encryption and de-identification. Cross-session long-term memory and profile entries are not itemised officially (2026-11-21).

Permissions & limits

Accounts log in with a StepFun ID; the official privacy summary lists microphone, camera and photo permissions and their scenarios. Conversation data can be used for training after de-identification, and users can manage it through privacy settings and feedback channels; exact switch paths should be checked in-app (2026-11-21).

Best for

Fit: a domestic chat product linked to an open-weight ecosystem, with same-vendor HF weights available for local deployment and direct mainland access. Not fit: leaving the StepFun account or running local models inside the chat product (2026-11-21).

Licence and terms of use

Free with login (some capabilities need a subscription). Governed by the official privacy policy dated 2026-11-02 and the user agreement; output ownership and training terms follow the original text (2026-11-21).

Top misconception

  • The Step Chat product and the Step open weights are two lines — the chat product does not run local weights; the weights line self-deploys via the HF org page (quantized entries on the models site)
  • The filing number's subject is the "JieYue" LLM (Shanghai-JieYue-20231106, filed 2023-11-24); a separate "JieYue multimodal" filing exists (2025-06-09) — which filing covers which multimodal capability should be read per public batch
  • www.stepfun.com is the vendor portal (a pure JS shell on the day; the product is seen via /chat) — do not mistake the portal for the chat entry
