A general chat app strong on long-form and writing quality: memory is an editable per-item list, and the Cowork line put “pause and resume after shutdown, scheduled tasks” into official release notes.

When to use it, when not to

Long-document analysis, writing and content production. If training matters, opt out in settings (the safety-review exception is explicitly written by the official side); enterprise data goes through the API/Enterprise line.

Not a fit for coding-agent needs — that is a separate product line (Claude Code) with its own profile.

Model & openness

The model family is disclosed via official release notes and routed by tier (2026 line: release records for Claude Fable 5.1 / Mythos 5.1 / Opus 5 / Sonnet 5 / Haiku 5.5 etc.); models are selectable in chat, and Enterprise admins can control which models members may use.

Where it runs

Web, desktop (macOS/Windows), mobile apps; chat generation is processed on Anthropic's cloud. The Cowork line has a local-session form (runs on your computer) alongside cloud sessions — this dossier's main axis is the chat app.

Local files

Can upload documents for long-document analysis; uploaded content is stored in the Claude account. Deleted chats disappear from the front end immediately and are deleted from the back end within 30 days (official privacy policy).

Tasks after shutdown

Cowork remote sessions (beta) officially state "Work continues when you close your laptop, and scheduled tasks run with no device online" (release notes 2026-07-07) — offline continuation and scheduled tasks have an official source.

Tools & extensions

Connector ecosystem (Microsoft 365 incl. write tools, Salesforce plugin and other official entries); Artifacts produce code and content; Skills/plugin security scanning (Enterprise beta).

Context & memory

Memory is a categorized-entry system: Settings > Memory shows all Topics with per-entry edit or delete; sensitive topics are kept out of memory by default; on by default for Free/Pro/Max, off by default for Team/Enterprise (release notes 2026-08-25).

Permissions & limits

Official privacy policy (effective 2026-09-10): consumer chats may be used for training by default; you can opt out in account settings; even after opting out, chats flagged by safety review are still used; API/Enterprise data is governed by customer agreements and this policy does not apply.

Best for

For people who value long-document reading and writing quality. For "chats never used for training", opt out in settings (note the safety-review exception); for contract-grade data terms go API/Enterprise. It is two product lines with Claude Code — don't conflate them.

Licence and terms of use

Official Consumer Terms of Service: as between you and Anthropic you retain rights in Inputs, and 'we assign to you all of our right, title, and interest—if any—in Outputs' (subject to compliance). The terms also state Outputs may not be unique.

When something fails

Account and permissions · Rate limits and retries · File-reading issues

Top misconception

  • Treating the chat app and Claude Code as one product line — two lines, two profiles
  • Assuming the training opt-out covers everything — conversations flagged by safety review are an explicitly documented exception
  • Treating memory as an uncontrollable black box — the official side provides per-item view/edit/delete controls
