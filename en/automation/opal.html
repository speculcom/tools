A no-code AI mini-app builder from Google Labs: natural-language steps chained generation, search and video into a shareable application. It shut down on 2026-11-17; workflows and shared links no longer run. Drive files remain, and prompts can be copied into Gemini skills or AI Studio and rebuilt for the destination task.

When to use it, when not to

No longer suitable for any new task. It used to fit non-coders who wanted to prototype and share apps chaining generation, search and video. It did not fit self-hosting, direct model choice or long-term production; the experimental service shut down on 2026-11-17.
