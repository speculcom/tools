Otter is the bot-joins-the-meeting transcription & notes product: real-time transcription + action items + cross-meeting Q&A, mature for English; the bot entering your meeting is itself a compliance question.

When to use it, when not to

Fits: English meetings/interviews, small-team note pipelines. Not a fit: Chinese meetings as the main use, privacy-critical settings, treating the free tier as a long-term plan.

Local files

Meeting recordings/transcripts/minutes stored on the account-side cloud; exports (txt/csv/srt etc.) count as local. No officially stated local-processing form seen.

Tasks after shutdown

Live bot-joined meeting transcription is the core form (a bot resides in the meeting); batch upload alongside; no "keep-running-after-shutdown" concept (cloud service).

Tools & extensions

Live transcription + speaker diarization + auto minutes/action items (official product surface), Otter Chat (ask questions of the transcript, official product surface), collaborative editing and sharing; the enterprise side has an admin console (seen on the official site).

Context & memory

A cross-meeting knowledge base is the product narrative (past meetings searchable/answerable) — that is its "memory" surface, details kept at official category level.

Permissions & limits

Account + subscription + enterprise system (D9: pricing not collected); the free tier has a monthly transcription-minutes cap (official pricing-page narrative, exact quota kept at official category level); meeting recordings involve multiple parties' privacy — informed consent for a bot joining is the user's responsibility (terms stance kept at official category level).

Best for

Fits: meeting/interview documentation (mature for English), transcription-minutes pipelines for individuals and small teams. Does not fit: Chinese meetings as the mainstay (the transcription engine has an English-leaning narrative), extremely privacy-sensitive scenarios (cloud + bot joining), treating the free tier as a long-term plan (minute limits).
