Moyin Workshop is Mobvoi's Chinese dubbing workbench: multi-voice text-to-speech + voice cloning + video-dubbing workflows, algorithm filing on record; domestic direct access and Chinese support are its convenience surface.

When to use it, when not to

Fits: Chinese dubbing workflows (audiobooks/short video/courseware), own-voice cloning, domestic direct access. Not a fit: cloning others (red line), open weights, English-first work.

8 questions, one table

8 questions, each comparable item by item with its peers.

Model & openness

Mobvoi’s in-house TTS line: the official tutorial page cites a fifth-generation TTS engine and 48 kHz output. Voice cloning comes in Efficient (20 recorded sentences, hours), Quality (100+ audio clips and transcripts, 2–3 working days) and Custom (500 professional sentences, 5–7 working days) tiers, with API access (2026-11-21).

Where it runs

The moyin.com web app is the main entry, alongside mobile apps, a mini program and the “Sequence Monkey” open-platform API; synthesis runs in the vendor cloud. Sentence-by-sentence audition, selection audition and full-text synthesis all happen in the browser (2026-11-21).

Local files

Albums and articles live account-side in the cloud, and albums do not share data; finished audio downloads as mp3 after “save and generate audio file”. Mobile and mini-program clients share the account system (2026-11-21).

Tasks after shutdown

Interactive editing plus synthesis: long articles take longer to render, and failures are retried by contacting support or resubmitting. The workflow centres on sentence-level editing, audition and re-synthesis, without a pauseable cloud job queue (2026-11-21).

Tools & extensions

Text dubbing (multi-voice, emotion, speed), sentence audition, polyphone/prosody/number correction, pause and silence insertion (600 ms default between paragraphs), multi-role dubbing (voice per segment), voice conversion, “voice crafting”, cloud video editing and one-click video parsing (2026-11-21).

Context & memory

“Albums” provide project context: each album has admin, chief editor and editor roles, and data and operations belong only to the current album, which can be switched in real time. This is team-workspace isolation rather than conversational memory, with voice assets providing cross-article consistency (2026-11-21).

Permissions & limits

Accounts plus album-admin billing: synthesis charges the current album admin’s balance, and insufficient funds block synthesis. Voice cloning requires a membership for editor use, with cloning and commercial rights under the official agreement; the agreement prohibits illegal cloning of TTS data and infringement of voice rights (2026-11-21).

Best for

Fit: Chinese dubbing pipelines (audiobooks, short video, courseware), multi-user album collaboration, cloning one’s own voice and domestic direct access. Not fit: cloning others (a consent red line), open weights/offline deployment, or English-first work (2026-11-21).

Licence and terms of use

Free trial plus subscription. Governed by the official user agreement (moyin.com/protocol, fetched 2026-11-21); voice cloning requires membership and compliance with consent terms (2026-11-21).
