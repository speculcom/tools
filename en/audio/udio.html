Udio is the other half of the music-generation duo (v1.5 Allegro line, complex-genre reputation), now mid-transition from copyright defendant to licensed platform — UMG/WMG have settled, the new platform goes walled-garden.

When to use it, when not to

Fits: music creation experience (complex genres), tracking the licensed-platform new form. Not a fit: commercial pipelines that download and carry away (walled-garden direction), needing stable terms (transition period).

8 questions, one table

8 questions, each comparable item by item with its peers.

Model & openness

An in-house model line (v1/v1.5/v1.5 Allegro, named as current in the official FAQ); the new platform will train new models on licensed and authorised music (UMG announcement). Official pages list no open-weight line, so models are tied to the platform (2026-11-21).

Where it runs

Web (udio.com), with generation in the vendor cloud; the official Guide describes a prompt→Create web flow, and paid tiers can generate 2/4/10 songs at once. The Discord beta is community history, while the current product surface is the web app (2026-11-21).

Local files

Works live in the account cloud; the new platform moves toward a walled garden, with the official announcement stating that AI-generated music cannot be downloaded or distributed outside the platform (UMG announcement). Transition-period downloads follow the current ToS (2026-11-21).

Tasks after shutdown

A task model: submit → queue in the cloud → land in the account library. The official Guide says the prompt box does not reset after Create, making it easy to queue further generations, and generation does not depend on the local machine being online. Official pages offer no pause, resume or queue-control panel (2026-11-21).

Tools & extensions

Creation tools include generate, extend, remix, inpainting, Styles and seed (named in the official Guide and FAQ); the new platform direction adds remix/covers and participating-artist voice styles (WMG announcement) (2026-11-21).

Context & memory

Task-based, with no conversational memory; style continuity comes from explicit mechanisms such as Styles, remix, extend and seed, which the official Guide lists as editing tools. Cross-session preferences and user profiles are not listed officially (2026-11-21).

Permissions & limits

The copyright settlements are central context: UMG settled and signed a licensing deal on 2025-10-29 and WMG settled on 2025-11-19 (official announcements); Merlin/Kobalt follow-ups are reported by trade media. Sony litigation continues. Output rights shift with the transition ToS and new-platform terms, so check the current ToS before commercial use (2026-11-21).

Best for

Fit: the creative experience of one of the two big music generators, with a reputation for complex styles, and tracking the licensed-platform transition. Not fit: a download-and-keep commercial pipeline (the walled-garden direction) or deterministic terms during the transition (2026-11-21).

Licence and terms of use

Free plus subscription during the transition. Governed by the official ToS (fetched 2026-10-22); a transition ToS took effect on 2025-11-12 (per the official FAQ) — output rights follow the transition ToS until the new licensed platform launches.

Top misconception

  • Udio is mid-transition from copyright defendant to licensed platform — any old 'Udio outputs are commercial' tutorial may be stale; check the current ToS before use
  • Walled garden is the new-platform direction (UMG announcement wording) — assuming 'generate = own + download' misjudges the future form
