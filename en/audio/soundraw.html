SOUNDRAW is a royalty-free AI beat generator pitching “trained only on music we create in-house” (SOUNDRAW Inc., on record same day): pick/edit instrumental tracks by mood/genre/length with a per-track Mixer, aimed at video creators.

When to use it, when not to

Fits: royalty-free scoring for video/podcasts, fast pick-and-edit by mood/genre/length, a “self-claimed clean training data” copyright narrative. Not a fit: direct mainland access with a filing, vocal songs with lyrics (instrumental beats are the pitch), running models locally.

Model & openness

Its own generation line: the same-day site headline wording is "trained only on music we create in-house" (the 100% Copyright-Safe narrative) — training data self-described as an in-house catalog; no third-party model picking.

Where it runs

Web product (soundraw.io); generation and the Mixer run in SOUNDRAW's cloud, operated from a browser, with no local model. API and desktop surfaces are not part of the product surface listed on the official site.

Local files

Downloaded exported audio lands on your machine; generation, the account library and Mixer projects live in SOUNDRAW's cloud. The product surface has no local model or offline generation entry.

Tasks after shutdown

A request is generated in the cloud; the official narrative uses "in seconds" for output speed, and closing the browser does not change the cloud job. The product surface offers no pause, resume or queue-position control.

Tools & extensions

Editing surface on record (per-track Mixer toggles/intensity/length, Blend Genres style mixing); MCP and a public API are not part of the capability surface listed on the official site, and extension interfaces are not itemised.

Context & memory
