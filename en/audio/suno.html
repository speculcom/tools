Model & openness

Model iterations disclosed via the official blog: the "Introducing v6" entry (2026-09-09, signed by Mikey Shulman, title and date verified via proxied direct fetch 2026-10-21); the pricing-page snapshot's model labels (v4.5-all/v5) lag behind the blog's v6 entry — the current default model follows what the logged-in app shows, this site does not adjudicate.

Where it runs

Web and mobile apps as main entries; generation processed on Suno's cloud, no model runs locally. Suno Studio is an in-browser generative workstation (multitrack timeline, stem export), still a cloud form — the interface lives in the browser, the compute in the cloud.

Local files

Supports uploading audio as reference/continuation (paid tiers allow longer uploads, see the pricing-page pointer); ownership of uploaded material is in the permissions axis.

Tasks after shutdown

Generation is task-based: submit prompts/material, it queues and produces, outputs enter the account library. No official statement of a "keep-running-after-shutdown" form — tasks run in the cloud, independent of your machine being online.

Tools & extensions

Suno Studio (web-based generative audio workstation: multitrack, stem export) and the editing-tools line (blog entries Studio 2.0, Speech (beta) 2026-10-01 etc.); Persona/Inspo and other style controls.

Context & memory

Generation is task-based, no cross-session conversation-memory form; style continuity works through Persona and similar mechanisms.

Permissions & limits

Official ToS: users retain ownership of Submissions ("you retain all right, title, and interest in and to your Submissions"), but grant Suno a worldwide royalty-free license (including creating derivative works); the terms explicitly cover using them to "train, develop, fine-tune or otherwise improve" its models, and no training opt-out switch is seen. Training other models with Outputs is prohibited by the terms.

Best for

People making AI music who care about "uploaded material stays mine". Commercial use must go through the paid download channel (see the license column); Remixes are non-commercial across the board; if you care about "material never entering training" — the terms have no opt-out option, you must accept that first.

Licence and terms of use

Official ToS: on Pro/Premier paid tiers, 'Suno hereby assigns to you all of its right, title and interest in and to any Output owned by Suno'; on the free/Basic tier, Outputs may only be used for 'lawful, personal and non-commercial purposes'. Commercial rights tie to 'Permitted Commercial Use' — only Outputs downloaded through your paid tier's download allocation may be commercially exploited. Remixes are non-commercial regardless of tier.

When something fails

Account and permissions · Rate limits and retries · File-reading issues

Top misconception

  • Reading “paid means commercial” precisely — commercial use is tied to the act of downloading via the paid-download quota, and Remix output is always non-commercial
  • Assuming uploaded material is taken away — you keep ownership of Submissions, but the platform obtains a broad license including training
  • Training other models on outputs — explicitly prohibited by the terms
