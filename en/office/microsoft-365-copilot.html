Microsoft 365 Copilot is an office and writing product for documents, sheets and presentations inside the existing Microsoft 365 workflow; it does not replace final legal or financial review.

When to use it, when not to

Fit: Microsoft 365 teams drafting documents, summarising email, following up meetings and getting spreadsheet help. Not fit: a cross-organisation general search engine; offline work or legal/financial final output.

8 questions, one table

8 questions, each comparable item by item with its peers.

Model & openness

Driven by Microsoft Copilot; the specific model and tier vary by Microsoft 365 edition, region and enterprise contract, and the product page does not name a single model.

Where it runs

Runs in Microsoft 365’s cloud, with entry points in Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook, Teams and the Copilot app; the local client handles the interface and document editing.

Local files

Can read documents, email, meetings and chat context in OneDrive and SharePoint; whether an unuploaded local file enters context depends on the client and tenant policy.

Tasks after shutdown

Meeting notes and email summaries are triggered inside the relevant app; there is no general “keep running after shutdown” background agent, and long work stays within a session.

Tools & extensions

Deep integration with Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook, Teams and SharePoint; Copilot Studio extends custom tools and connectors.

Context & memory

Context comes from Microsoft Graph and tenant content the user can access; cross-app memory is bounded by tenant permissions, retention policy and user authorisation.

Permissions & limits

Tenant admins control availability, data boundary and logs; commercial use depends on the Microsoft 365 contract and Copilot licence, not the personal-edition description.

Best for

Fits: document drafting, email summaries, meeting follow-up and spreadsheet assistance for teams already in Microsoft 365. Does not fit: treating it as a cross-organisation general search engine.

See the official product and terms pages; this entry records capabilities and boundaries only, not price figures or the full contract.
