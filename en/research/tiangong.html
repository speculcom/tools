Tiangong grew from "AI search" into a "super agent": search as the base, with documents/PPT/spreadsheets/webpages generated directly in multiple modes, plus template reuse ("make the same"); reachable directly from the mainland.

When to use it, when not to

Chinese search plus office artifacts in one chain (PPT/spreadsheet/document generated directly), template-reuse workflows, mainland direct access. Not for: English-first retrieval, delivering generated artifacts without human review (PPTs/spreadsheets need checking).
