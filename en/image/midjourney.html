Model & openness

Model iterations are disclosed via the official updates page (updates.midjourney.com) and the Alpha changelog (2026-10-08 "Testing Thinking Mode", 9-24 "Edit updates, thumbnail previews" and other entries, direct-fetched and verified 2026-10-21); versions are selectable at generation time, the default version follows the official docs.

Where it runs

Two usage lines: the web app and the Discord bot; generation processed on Midjourney's cloud, no models run locally.

Local files

Supports image uploads as reference and editing input; outputs are public and browsable in the community by default and can be remixed by others (official help-page framing); only the Pro/Mega tiers' Stealth mode promises best-effort non-publicity.

Tasks after shutdown

Generation is task-based (submit-queue-output); no official statement of a "keep-running-after-shutdown" form.

Tools & extensions

The web editor keeps iterating (edit updates, thumbnail previews and other changelog entries); the Discord bot line can generate inside servers; the Remix mechanism is the product's built-in path for derivative creation.

Context & memory

Generation is task-based, no cross-session memory form; personalization shows through parameters and Remix.

Permissions & limits

Official privacy policy §1 explicitly applies to "data collected through the process of training Midjourney's machine-learning algorithms"; the official AB2013 training-data disclosure states the training set includes "data provided by third parties and Midjourney users"; public and remixable by default, Stealth mode (Pro/Mega) is the best-effort non-publicity option; no user-facing training opt-out switch found in official docs.

Best for

People who weight image style quality highly and can accept the "public by default" community form. If you need works kept private or clear commercial ownership, read the subscription tiers and Stealth clauses before starting; large-revenue companies should note the revenue-threshold clause.

Licence and terms of use

Official ToS §4 'Content Rights': paid users own their Assets — 'You own all Assets You create with the Services to the fullest extent possible under applicable law', and ownership 'persists even if in subsequent months You downgrade or cancel Your membership'. Exceptions: companies (or employees) with over $1,000,000 USD annual revenue must hold Pro/Mega to own Assets; upscaling another user's image leaves it with the original creator. Non-paid members receive a CC Noncommercial 4.0 license instead of ownership. Users grant Midjourney a perpetual royalty-free license over Content and Assets.

When something fails

Account and permissions · Rate limits and retries · File-reading issues

Top misconception

  • Reading “paid users own the Assets” as unconditional — companies with annual revenue over USD 1M only own Assets on the Pro/Mega tier
  • Treating Stealth as a training opt-out — it only governs public visibility; the official docs have no training opt-out switch
  • Assuming cancelling a subscription forfeits ownership of past outputs — the official docs state ownership continues after downgrade/cancellation
