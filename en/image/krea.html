8 questions, one table

8 questions, each comparable item by item with its peers.

Model & openness

Multi-model aggregation + in-house hybrid: the official site lists 64+ models (Veo/Ideogram/Runway/Luma/Flux/Gemini etc.); the in-house Krea 2 line; a real-time generation line. The model list changes with plans and launches, so the signed-in catalogue is authoritative.

Where it runs

Web (krea.ai); generation runs in Krea's cloud and the real-time canvas is operated in the browser. There is no official offline client or local weight download; low latency is the selling point, and no concrete SLA or concurrency figure is listed publicly.

Local files

Works and projects are stored account-side in the cloud, with downloads landing locally. Whether LoRA training artefacts can be exported locally is not itemised on public pages; the product surface has no local inference or offline generation entry.

Tasks after shutdown

Real-time generation is the selling point (output as you type/draw); batch or long jobs queue in the cloud, and the product surface has no pause, resume or task-position control. Shutting the client down does not change the cloud job.

Tools & extensions

Real-time canvas, LoRA fine-tuning, an upscaling line, video-model aggregation (Veo/Runway etc.); an API surface exists but public documentation is limited, and MCP is not part of the listed capability surface.

Context & memory

Organised by projects/boards; the product surface has no cross-session memory entry. The real-time canvas's "current frame" is in-session state, not persistent memory.

Permissions & limits

Account + subscription system; the Terms of Use (2024-05-20 version) grant Krea a broad licence over User Submissions, and commercial rights vary by tier. Marketing says "Do not train"; the binding constraint is the privacy-page text.

Best for

Fits: real-time interactive creation, switching between multiple vendors' models on one platform, cloud LoRA training. Does not fit: wanting open weights or offline use, deep per-model parameter control, or treating aggregation as "best at everything".

Licence and terms of use

Free + subscription. Governed by the official privacy/terms pages (krea.ai/privacy, direct-fetched 2026-10-22); commercial rights vary by plan, training clauses not verified line by line.
