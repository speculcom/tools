SeaArt is the community-type anime/illustration aggregation console: cloud LoRA training with ComfyUI export, a community model library whose licenses are labeled per model, multi-vendor model aggregation; a going-overseas product by Chengdu-based Haiyi.

When to use it, when not to

Fits: anime/illustration community ecosystem, cloud LoRA training then export to ComfyUI, community model treasure-hunting. Not a fit: needing official frontier self-developed models (it aggregates), enterprise compliance narratives.

8 questions, one table

8 questions, each comparable item by item with its peers.

Model & openness

Multi-model aggregation platform: the official site lists third-party models (Seedance/Wan/GPT Image/Seedream/Flux/Qwen Image etc., as seen in site navigation and the privacy page) + a community model library (checkpoint/LoRA hosting; model pages mark Base Model and license scope); an in-house model line — official docs do not state it.

Where it runs

Web (seaart.ai / seaart.me) + app (an APP entry on the official site); generation on the vendor's cloud. Community models can be downloaded (model pages have a Download surface) — running them locally after download goes through the local ecosystem; SeaArt does not provide a local runtime.

Local files

Works and models stored account-side in the cloud; community models downloadable to local (Download surface on model pages). No officially stated local data surface — downloaded model files belong to the local filesystem, unrelated to the platform.

Tasks after shutdown

Generation is instant/queued (queuing at peak hours is a common community feedback; tier differences official docs do not itemize every sub-detail); no "keep-running-after-shutdown" task surface (no claim verified); LoRA training runs on the cloud after submission.

Tools & extensions

Online LoRA training (official page: upload a dataset, pick a base model FLUX/SDXL/Pony/Wan etc., exportable to interoperate with ComfyUI), ControlNet/inpainting, image+video dual lines (as seen in site navigation), a community gallery (works shared with prompts), a model-marketplace monetization surface (official FAQ framing).

Context & memory

Organized by artwork/model pages; no cross-session memory entry. The community model library is its consistency mechanism — reusable style models are not memory; style models can be reused across works.

Permissions & limits

Account + subscription system (D9: prices not collected); commercial rights labeled per model (License Scope surface on model pages); the training-use switch not itemized in official docs line by line. The entity is a Chinese team's going-overseas product (high share of overseas users; the main site targets overseas).

Best for

Fits: anime/illustration community ecosystem, cloud LoRA training + export to ComfyUI, aggregating multiple vendors' models on one platform, downloading community models. Does not fit: wanting official in-house frontier models (it is an aggregator), enterprise-grade compliance narratives, scenarios sensitive to community content moderation.

Licence and terms of use

Free + subscription. Governed by the official terms page (seaart.ai/en/terms, direct-fetched 2026-10-22); commercial rights vary by plan and per-model license — community model pages state reposted models' rights belong to original creators, and commercial scope of generated images is labeled per model; details not verified line by line.
