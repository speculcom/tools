Model & openness

Model family disclosed on the official news page: the Stable Image series (platform), Stable Audio 3.0 (released 2026-05-20, open weights, officially claimed fully licensed training data), the Stable Diffusion 3.5 series (self-hostable) and other entries (stability.ai/news direct-fetched and verified 2026-10-21).

Where it runs

Platform line: web app and API, generation in the cloud; open line: weights downloadable, self-hosted on your machine or your own cloud — the two lines are two runtime forms of the same vendor; this dossier's main axis is the platform.

Local files

The platform can upload images as input; on the open line, when the model runs locally the data never leaves your machine (an inherent property of the self-hosted form).

Tasks after shutdown

Platform generation is request-output based: tasks run in the cloud and are retrieved on completion; no official statement of a "keep-running-after-shutdown" form. On the open line, self-hosted tasks are carried entirely by your own machines — keep-running-after-shutdown depends on your own deployment.

Tools & extensions

The platform includes Brand Studio (2026-04-08 entry: a brand-oriented creative production platform); the enterprise-integration line (Amazon Bedrock 2025-09-18 entry, NVIDIA NIM 2025-08-12 entry); the open-source ecosystem (ComfyUI and other third-party tools) is out of this dossier's scope.

Context & memory

Generation is task-based, no cross-session memory form; each generation is independent, style continuity relies on prompts and in-platform asset libraries (Brand Studio materials), not session memory.

Permissions & limits

Official ToS: users can opt out of having their content used for training (the opt-out mechanism is written into the terms); the platform takes operationally necessary licenses over uploaded content. Training-data stance for the open-weight models is in the official model cards and benchmark reports.

Best for

People who want both lines within reach: "platform service + self-hostable open models". Commercial terms differ between the platform and open versions — read them separately; scenarios sensitive to training-data compliance may note its audio line's "fully licensed data" stance.

Licence and terms of use

Official ToS §4(a): 'we assign to you all of our right, title, and interest (if any) in the Outputs' — output ownership is assigned to the user. §3(b) limits: no reselling outputs, no training competing models on outputs, no removing watermarks or provenance marks. The open-weight Stable Diffusion models carry their own open licenses — commercial self-hosting follows those model licenses, a separate regime from the platform terms.

Account and permissions · Rate limits and retries · File-reading issues

Top misconception

  • Applying the platform ToS to self-hosted open models — two license systems, completely different commercial paths
  • Assuming open weights are freely commercial — licenses differ per model (community/enterprise license lines); check model by model
  • Remembering “training opt-out available” as Midjourney-style no-switch — Stability's opt-out is written into the terms
