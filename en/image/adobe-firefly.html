8 questions, one table

8 questions, each comparable item by item with its peers.

Model & openness

Adobe's own Firefly model family (image/video/sound multimodal lines) + optional third-party models inside the platform (official narrative: Firefly apps can call third-party models; not inventoried item by item); models are listed on the Adobe side.

Where it runs

Web (firefly.adobe.com) + generative capabilities embedded in CC apps such as Photoshop/Premiere (Generative Fill etc.) — this dossier covers the standalone Firefly product line; the CC-embedded line is not listed separately per the boundary ruling (see inventory notes).

Local files

Artwork lives in Adobe cloud (Creative Cloud storage); no local model execution — generation and the asset library stay on the cloud account, with sync and collaboration governed by account permissions.

Tasks after shutdown

Generation is instant/queued (the web queue varies by account tier, official docs do not itemize every sub-detail); no "keep-running-after-shutdown" task surface (no claim verified); the CC-app-embedded line runs in the app's foreground, not as a background agent.

Tools & extensions

The product includes text-to-image, Generative Fill/Expand, style reference, structure reference, vector recoloring (design-oriented), video generation (the Firefly video line), etc.; integration with the Adobe Stock/Express ecosystem is part of the official narrative.

Context & memory

Organized by projects/libraries (CC ecosystem: integration surfaces between Firefly works and CC asset libraries exist; details official docs do not itemize every sub-detail); cross-session memory — official docs do not state it.

Permissions & limits

Adobe account + CC subscription system; commercial indemnification is the enterprise-facing selling point (long-standing official position); training-data stance = licensed content library (long-standing official position) — these two points are its biggest compliance differentiator from most peers.

Best for

Fits: enterprise compliance procurement (indemnification + licensed-training narrative), users already in the Adobe ecosystem, design-to-delivery pipelines. Does not fit: leaving the Adobe account system; wanting open weights to run locally (Firefly is not open source).

Licence and terms of use

Free allowance + Creative Cloud subscription. Governed by Adobe's general terms + Firefly-specific terms (adobe.com/legal/terms.html, direct-fetched 2026-10-22); Adobe's long-standing official position: Firefly is trained on licensed content and enterprise customers can receive output indemnification — clause details not verified line by line.
