The dual-route supplier of the FLUX model family: hosted generation via the official API/playground, some weights open for self-hosting and even on-device; “claims no ownership, but bans training other models” is the sharpest cut in the terms.

When to use it, when not to

Teams integrating the FLUX API or self-hosting open weights. Anyone doing model training reads 1.3(n) first — training other models on FLUX outputs is prohibited by the terms.

Not a fit for out-of-the-box creative-community workflows — compare Midjourney before choosing.

Model & openness

Model family with an iteration line disclosed on the official blog: FLUX 3 (2026-07-23 "Real World Models" multimodal flow-model entry), FLUX 3 Video Part 1 (2026-08-04), FLUX Video Upscale 2K/4K (2026-08-20), FLUX 3 Action (2026-09-23), FLUX 3 Image Klein (2026-10-15, 5.6B open-weights entry) — verified by direct fetch of bfl.ai/blog on 2026-10-21; some weights are open and self-hostable.

Where it runs

Hosted line: official API and playground, generation in the cloud; open line: weights downloadable for self-hosting, plus an official on-device entry (FLUX.2 Klein into ASUS ProArt laptops, 2026-06-04).

Local files

API/playground can upload images as input; on the self-hosted line, when the model runs locally the data never leaves your machine.

Tasks after shutdown

The API is request-output based: tasks run in the cloud, clients can disconnect and poll to retrieve results; no official statement of a "keep-running-after-shutdown" form — the model interface itself carries no cross-session tasks.

Tools & extensions

The main form is a model interface (API/playground) integrated by third-party apps and platforms (e.g. Runway, various image workflows); the official side does not build an end-user creation suite.

Context & memory

The model interface is stateless: each request is independent, with no cross-request memory; context continuity is organized by the caller's application layer (multi-reference-image input is an in-request capability, not session memory).

Permissions & limits

Official ToS: inputs and outputs may by default be used to improve the model, opting out requires an email request to legal@blackforestlabs.ai; the terms explicitly restrict "training other models on Outputs" (1.3(n)).

Best for

People/teams that want the official FLUX API, or want to self-host open FLUX weights. Hosted and self-hosted licensing paths differ — confirm which one you are on before commercial use; anyone doing model training should note the 1.3(n) output-training ban.

Licence and terms of use

Official ToS 1.2(a): the platform does not claim ownership of Outputs. 1.3(e): commercial use is limited to your own Outputs. 1.3(n): training other models on Outputs is prohibited. Open FLUX weights are released under their respective open licenses; commercial self-hosting follows those model licenses — a separate regime from the platform terms.

When something fails

Account and permissions · Rate limits and retries · File-reading issues

Top misconception

  • Reading “claims no ownership” as “commercialize freely” — commercial use is limited to your own Output, and 1.3(n) bans training other models
  • Applying hosted-API terms to self-hosted open weights — two license systems
  • The training opt-out is not a self-serve switch — email legal@ to request it
