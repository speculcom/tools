Meeting notes tools compared: Otter, Fireflies, tl;dv and Whisper

This page does not repeat “99% transcription accuracy” ads. The real differences are who can join your meeting, how much free quota you get, where data stays, and whether Chinese multi-speaker meetings work.

Start with the decision

Four situations, four choices

01

Zero budget; first test whether it works

Otter Basic gives 300 minutes/month and 30 minutes per session; Fireflies Free gives 400 minutes/team storage; tl;dv Free gives unlimited recording/transcription but only 10 AI notes/month. Check your meeting length and count, not the word “free”.

02

Chinese meetings; already working in Feishu

Try Feishu Smart Meeting. AI membership is ¥69/month with 3,000 ASR minutes; Plus is ¥138/month with 6,000 minutes. The trial has only two AI notes per person. Its advantage is the note living in a Feishu doc, not the ASR model itself.

03

Sales or recruiting calls that must sync to CRM

Fireflies or tl;dv fit better: CRM sync, snippet replay and team analytics are on paid tiers. Otter’s strength is transcription and Otter Chat, not CRM workflow.

04

Meeting content cannot leave the machine

Run Whisper locally under MIT. The official repository does not do speaker diarisation; multi-speaker meetings need WhisperX or pyannote, plus your own compute and segmentation.

What to verify

Before you commit

  • Confirm which meeting platforms the bot can join.
  • Check whether recording consent and retention meet your policy.
  • Test Chinese multi-speaker audio, not only a clean English sample.
  • Separate transcription minutes from AI-note or CRM quotas.
