AI search tools compared: Perplexity, Kagi and alternatives

This page does not rank “the smartest”. It answers four questions: where the sources come from, whether citations open, whether it works from mainland China, and what you pay each month.

Start with the decision

Four situations, four choices

01

Global English sources; willing to check every citation

Start with Perplexity Pro. Its citation workflow is the most complete of the four, but a 2025 Tow Center test still found errors in 37% of answers. A citation marker is not a fact-check.

02

Ad-free, independent index, paid privacy

Look at Kagi. Starter is $5/month for 300 searches; Professional is $10/month unlimited. The front-end product is non-commercial by default; read the API terms before commercial use.

03

Mainland direct access; Chinese web, academic and library sources

Metaso is the simpler route; Skywork fits “search, then produce a document, slide deck or table”. Both have free tiers, but this pass did not capture a citable price sheet, so this page does not invent numbers.

04

Do not treat “has citations” as “verified”

None of the four has a reproducible Chinese fact-checking benchmark. Use AI search for leads, open the citation, and verify the key claim yourself.

What to verify

Before you commit

  • Open the cited source; a snippet may omit the condition that changes the answer.
  • Check whether “China direct” means the consumer product or only an API.
  • Separate the subscription price from API or credit overage.
  • Test one real research task with a source you already know.
