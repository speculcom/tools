AI tool cost table: subscription, API, overage and exclusions

Break the monthly fee into five bills: seats, model usage, overage, storage/retention and human review. The table is a snapshot, not a quote.

Start with the bill

Five lines, not one monthly fee

01

Seats

Per person/month, not per usage. Count active seats; do not buy for the whole company on day one.

02

Model / usage

Third-party models, API tokens, AI credits and per-minute transcription are usually billed separately.

03

Overage

The unit price after the allowance is often higher than the plan. Find the “overage” row before you buy.

04

Storage / retention

Meeting recordings, cloud outputs and logs may have separate storage and retention charges.

05

Human review

Wrong transcripts, citations and code changes cost staff time. That time belongs in the bill too.

Do your own math

Subscription or API? Find the break-even

A rough rule: put “monthly API cost” and “seat subscription + overage” on the same sheet. If API cost is below about 60% of the subscription, start usage-based; if it is close to or above the subscription, the plan is usually less operational work. The real break-even depends on call volume, cache hit rate and human review time.

Excluded from the table: tax, FX, annual discounts, minimum seats, enterprise contracts, actual model-routing prices, storage/export, network and compliance, and human review. They can make a “cheap” option expensive.
