模型与开放条件Model & openness

万相（Wan）系列双轨。产品与 API 端覆盖参考生视频、图生视频、文生视频、文件或网页生视频、视频编辑、文生图、图像编辑与组图生成。

代次含 Wan2.5，原生多模态、音画同步、10 秒 1080p/24fps。Wan3.0 在阿里云产品页标 30 秒、四模态参考。

开源侧 Wan2.2 在 GitHub/HF 以 Apache-2.0 发布。TI2V-5B 可在 4090 级消费卡跑 720p/24fps（2026-11-21）。

The Wan family runs on two tracks: the product/API side covers reference-to-video, image-to-video, text-to-video, file/web-to-video, video editing, text-to-image, image editing and image-set generation, across generations including Wan2.5 (native multimodal, audio-video sync, 10 s at 1080p/24fps) and Wan3.0 (the Alibaba Cloud product page cites 30 s and four-modality references). The open side ships Wan2.2 on GitHub/HF under Apache-2.0, with TI2V-5B running 720p/24fps on a 4090-class consumer card (2026-11-21).

产品端素材与作品存阿里云账户。开源线权重、推理与生成物全在本机。两条线数据边界完全不同。API 结果 URL 有效期 24 小时，需自行转存（2026-11-21）。

Product-side assets and works live in the Alibaba Cloud account, while the open line keeps weights, inference and outputs entirely local. The two data boundaries are distinct: API result URLs last 24 hours and must be copied out (2026-11-21).

云端异步任务制。创建任务拿 task_id（查询有效期 24 小时），状态 PENDING→RUNNING→SUCCEEDED/FAILED。轮询取结果。

Cloud async tasks: creation returns a task_id valid for 24 hours for queries, states move PENDING → RUNNING → SUCCEEDED/FAILED, clients poll for results, and successful image/video URLs last 24 hours. The open line follows the local process, with no hosted resume (2026-11-21).
