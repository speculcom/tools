Krea
免费+订阅 实时生成 模型聚合 LoRA训练 自研线 partial lifecycle: active 核验Checked 2026-10-22

Krea 是「实时生成 + 多模型聚合」的创作台：边画边出的实时线是招牌，官网聚合 64+ 家模型 （Veo/Ideogram/Flux 等）+ 自研 Krea 2 线；「Do not train」是官方营销口径。

Krea is the real-time + multi-model aggregation console: draw-as-you-see realtime line is the signature, 64+ third-party models aggregated (Veo/Ideogram/Flux…) plus self-developed Krea 2; 'Do not train' is marketing copy, terms govern.

适合与不适合When to use it, when not to

适合：实时交互创作、一平台切换多模型、云端 LoRA 训练。 不适合：开放权重离线、单模型深度调参、把聚合当「全都最强」。

Fits: real-time interactive creation, switching among many models in one platform, cloud LoRA training without local setup. Not a fit: open weights, offline use, treating every aggregated model as best-in-class.
