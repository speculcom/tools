Jimeng is ByteDance's dedicated image/video creation console (Seedream/Seedance model lines, built by the Jianying team): text-to-image, image-to-video and digital-human lines in one console; the generative-AI service filing number was not found (only an ICP number verified) — filing: unknown is an honest gap, not a verdict.

适合与不适合When to use it, when not to

适合：中文语义出图、图视频一体、抖音发布链路。 不适合：要模型自选、要免费无水印、要本机跑模型。

Fits: domestic users' image+video one-stop creation, digital-human/short-video workflows, direct access without a network path. Not a fit: open weights, model self-selection, leaving the ByteDance account.
