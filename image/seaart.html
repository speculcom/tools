SeaArt 是二次元/插画向的社区型聚合创作台：云端 LoRA 训练+导出、社区模型库（许可逐模型标注）、 多家第三方模型聚合；中国团队（成都海艺互娱）的出海产品。

SeaArt is the community-type anime/illustration aggregation console: cloud LoRA training with ComfyUI export, a community model library whose licenses are labeled per model, multi-vendor model aggregation; a going-overseas product by Chengdu-based Haiyi.

适合与不适合When to use it, when not to

适合：二次元插画生态、云端训练 LoRA 再导 ComfyUI、社区模型寻宝、一平台多模型。 不适合：要官方自研前沿模型、企业合规叙事优先、对社区内容治理敏感。

Fits: anime/illustration community ecosystem, cloud LoRA training then export to ComfyUI, community model treasure-hunting. Not a fit: needing official frontier self-developed models (it aggregates), enterprise compliance narratives.
