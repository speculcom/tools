Playground 是「设计成品」向的 AI 设计台：模板+Smart Layers 图层编辑把点子变成贴纸/T 恤/海报/logo， 生图用第三方模型（GPT Image 2/Nano Banana/Seedream）；自研模型时代已结束，公司 2026-06 售出后由新团队运营。

Playground is an AI design bench aimed at finished designs: templates + Smart Layers editing turn ideas into stickers, T-shirts, posters and logos, with third-party models (GPT Image 2 / Nano Banana / Seedream) doing the generation; the in-house model era is over, and since the June 2026 sale a new team runs it.

适合：成品设计流（周边/海报/logo）、模板+非破坏性图层编辑、一界面多家模型对比。 不适合：要自研模型线、要输出版权确定性、企业合规叙事优先。

Fits: finished-design flows (merch/posters/logos), template + non-destructive layer editing, comparing several models in one UI. Not a fit: needing an in-house model line, needing output-copyright certainty, enterprise compliance narratives.
