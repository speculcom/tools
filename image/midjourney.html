Midjourney, Inc.
云端 社区公开 Discord 商业 partial lifecycle: active 核验Checked 2026-10-21

图像生成风格质量的行业标杆：网页 + Discord 双线，社区默认公开可 Remix； 「付费拥有 Assets、但大营收公司要高档位」是最容易被忽略的一条许可门槛。

The industry benchmark for image-generation style quality: web + Discord dual line, community output public and Remixable by default; “paid users own the Assets, but high-revenue companies need a higher tier” is the licensing threshold most overlooked.

适合与不适合When to use it, when not to

适合重图像风格质量、接受「默认公开」社区形态的创作者。要作品不公开就查 Stealth 条款 （Pro/Mega 专属、尽力性质）；要商用归属清晰先读 ToS §4 与订阅档；公司用户注意营收例外。 在意「素材绝不进训练」——官方文档没有训练退出开关，这一点要先接受。

Creators who care about image style quality and accept the “public by default” community model. To keep work private, check the Stealth clause (Pro/Mega only, best-effort in nature); for clear commercial ownership read ToS §4 and the subscription tier; corporate users mind the revenue exception.

Not a fit for “materials never trained on” — the official docs have no training opt-out switch; accept that first.

头号误解Top misconception

  • 把「付费拥有 Assets」当成无门槛——年营收超 100 万美元的公司须 Pro/Mega 档才拥有Reading “paid users own the Assets” as unconditional — companies with annual revenue over USD 1M only own Assets on the Pro/Mega tier
  • 把 Stealth 当成训练退出——它只管公开与否，官方文档没有训练 opt-out 开关Treating Stealth as a training opt-out — it only governs public visibility; the official docs have no training opt-out switch
  • 以为取消订阅后失去产出所有权——官方明写所有权在降级/取消后延续Assuming cancelling a subscription forfeits ownership of past outputs — the official docs state ownership continues after downgrade/cancellation
