Microsoft Designer 是微软生态的免费 AI 设计入口（模板+生成一体，模型由微软路由）： 与 Copilot 图像线同生态不同入口；商用权随订阅档位变化。

Microsoft Designer is the free design entry of the Microsoft ecosystem (templates + generation): models routed on Microsoft's side; same ecosystem as Copilot's image line but a different entry; commercial rights vary by subscription tier.

适合与不适合When to use it, when not to

适合：微软生态轻量设计产出、免费起步、模板+AI 混合。 不适合：要模型自选、要开放权重、要脱离微软账号。

Fits: Microsoft-ecosystem lightweight design (social graphics/posters), free start. Not a fit: model self-selection, open weights, leaving the Microsoft account.
