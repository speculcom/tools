NotebookLM 是「源锚定」的研究工作台：你给一组源，它只在这些源里回答+生成音频概览/学习指南； 它不是搜索引擎——是资料集上的问答引擎。

A source-anchored research workbench: you hand it a set of sources and it answers only inside them, plus audio overviews and study guides — it is not a search engine but a Q&A engine over your material.

适合与不适合When to use it, when not to

适合：给定资料集内问答（论文组/尽调/课程）、长材料转音频、源引用可追溯。 不适合：开放网络检索、大陆直连（000）。

Q&A and research organization inside a given source set (paper groups, diligence packs, course material), turning long material into audio overviews, "answers only from your sources" scenarios. Not for: open-web retrieval (it does no web search — the sources are yours), mainland direct access (measured 000).
