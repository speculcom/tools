Consensus 回答「科学界怎么说」：Consensus Meter 把多篇研究的结论极性统计成摘要—— 快速判断实证领域共识方向；但计数不等权，Meter 不是定论。

Consensus answers "what does the scientific literature say": the Consensus Meter tallies conclusion polarity across studies into a summary — a fast read on empirical consensus, but the count is unweighted and the Meter is not a verdict.

适合与不适合When to use it, when not to

适合：实证领域共识速查、文献初筛、结论极性判断。 不适合：深度综述（抽取轻于 Elicit）、中文文献、把 Meter 当答案终点。

"What does science say" questions (medical/nutrition/climate and other empirical fields), fast conclusion-polarity reads, literature first-pass screening. Not for: deep systematic reviews (the extraction layer is lighter than Elicit), Chinese literature as the main axis, treating the Meter as settled truth.
