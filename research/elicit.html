Elicit
免费+订阅 学术检索 结构化抽取 系统性综述 英文向 partial lifecycle: active 核验Checked 2026-10-22

Elicit 是学术文献的「检索+结构化抽取」工作台：研究问题→论文筛选→抽取表→带引用综述—— 系统性综述流程的 AI 化；每个单元格仍要回原文核。

An academic-literature "retrieve + structured extraction" workbench: research question → paper screening → extraction tables → cited synthesis — a systematic-review pipeline, AI-assisted; every cell still needs checking against the original.

适合与不适合When to use it, when not to

适合：系统性综述/文献调研（英文文献池）、抽取表工作流。 不适合：中文文献主打、非学术检索、要全文获取。

Systematic reviews and literature surveys (medical/social-science/ML English literature pools), researchers who want extraction tables rather than abstract-reading. Not for: Chinese literature as the main axis (the academic pool is English-facing), non-academic retrieval, full-text acquisition (it anchors on abstracts and available full-text snippets).

头号误解Top misconception

  • 「AI 帮你读文献」≠「AI 结论可信」——抽取表的每个单元格要回原文核，尤其数值类"AI reads papers for you" is not "AI conclusions are trustworthy" — every cell of the extraction table needs checking against the original, especially numbers
  • 检索覆盖取决于底层学术库——不在库里的论文检索不到，系统性综述要多库交叉Retrieval coverage depends on the underlying academic database — papers outside it cannot be found; systematic reviews need multi-database cross-checks
