Devv 是开发者向的 AI 搜索问答：代码问题→实时抓官方文档→带链接答案； 它答代码问题但不改代码库——与 coding 大类的边界就在这条线上。

A developer-facing AI search Q&A: coding questions → live fetch of official docs → answers with links; it answers code questions but does not touch your codebase — that is the boundary with the coding category.
