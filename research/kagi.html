Kagi 是「付费换隐私」的自建索引搜索引擎：无广告无追踪是主轴，AI 答案层（FastGPT/Teclis） 是附加——它与「AI 搜索」的顺序正好相反。

A self-built-index search engine where you pay for privacy: no ads and no tracking is the main axis, the AI answer layer (FastGPT/Teclis) is the add-on — the exact reverse order of "AI search".

适合与不适合When to use it, when not to

适合：付费换无追踪体验、「搜索+全文读」答案、对追踪敏感用户。 不适合：免费用户、中文检索主打、依赖国内支付面。

Users willing to pay for ad-free/tracking-free search, "search + full-text reading" style answers (the Teclis line), privacy-sensitive users. Not for: free-first users (no free tier as the main axis), mainland direct-access sensitivity (direct 200 reachable but payment is overseas-style), Chinese retrieval as the main axis.
