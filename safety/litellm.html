本地文件Local files

读的是你本机的 config.yaml（模型清单、密钥引用、guardrail 配置），密钥与路由定义都在你自己的环境； 花费与日志写进你自管的 Postgres。它不读你的业务代码工作区，文件面就是配置与数据落库两处。

It reads your own config.yaml (model list, key references, guardrail settings); keys and routing definitions stay in your environment, and spend and logs go into the Postgres you manage. It does not read your application source tree - the file surface is configuration plus where you persist data.

It is a long-running gateway process: queueing, retries and routing happen service-side, and surviving a shutdown depends on how you deploy it (Helm or a VM). 上下文与记忆Context & memory

上下文由调用方透传，网关自身不做记忆或压缩。虚拟密钥、预算与团队是访问层的边界，不是上下文层； 上下文窗口实际大小由你路由到的模型决定。

Context passes through from the caller; the gateway itself keeps no memory and does no compression. Virtual keys, budgets and teams are access-layer boundaries, not context-layer ones, and the effective window is whatever the routed model gives you.

Access control is its core: the virtual-keys page records keys minted through /key/generate, model, budget, rate-limit and team scoping, a master key for administration, and scheduled rotation as an Enterprise capability. The guardrails page records that most integrations run in the open-source build while dynamic parameters and per-key rail control need Enterprise. Data flow follows whichever providers you attach. SSO and audit logs sit in the Enterprise framing (simple-proxy page). Compliance certifications and third-party audits were not found on official pages and are recorded as unknown.

适合什么任务Best for

适合要把多家 provider 收成一个 OpenAI 兼容入口、同时按 key / team 做预算与访问治理、并坚持自托管的团队。 不适合只要一个薄 SDK 的场景，也不适合把「厂商背书的托管护栏 + 合规承诺」当必需项的团队。

Fits: teams collapsing many providers into one OpenAI-compatible entry point, governing spend and access per key or team, and insisting on self-hosting. Not a fit: anyone wanting only a thin client SDK, nor anyone for whom a vendor-backed hosted guardrail with compliance attestations is a requirement - rails here are external services you plug in, not a vendor guarantee.

What it governs is on record: model access, budgets and rate limits, team membership, which MCP tools a principal can see, and where external rails attach. What it does not claim: any training-use promise about content safety, or published numbers for how well anything blocks. Per-key rail control and dynamic parameters sit in the Enterprise framing, so the open-source / Enterprise split has to be checked line by line against the official pages.
