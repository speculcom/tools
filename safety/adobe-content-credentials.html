适合什么任务Best for

适合发布方与平台把来源与编辑史做成可验证声明，也适合需要区分「没有凭证」与「凭证被改坏」两种状态的审计流程。 不适合要拦有害内容的场景（那是内容过滤那条线），也不适合没有任何签发方参与、却想凭空验真的期待。

Fits: publishers and platforms turning provenance and edit history into verifiable claims, and audit workflows that must separate 'no credentials present' from 'credentials tampered with'. Not a fit: anyone wanting harmful-content blocking (that is the content-filtering line) or expecting verification with no issuer involved at all. This site does not rank it against filtering products - the two lines answer different questions.

能力边界Capability boundaries

校验能做的是：验签名、比对内容绑定、检查被引用的 assertions、按 trust list 判断签发者可信度， 并明确区分「缺失」与「无效」。它不能保证内容不误导（本站观点）—— 编辑史真实，叙事仍可能骗人；官方页面只把校验范围写成签名、绑定与 assertions。 另有一条常被忽略：身份 assertion 与签名校验是分开绑定的， 所以「谁签的」和「谁声称自己是谁」在结论里是两件事（文档 hub 在册）。

What verification can do: check the signature, compare content bindings, inspect referenced assertions, judge issuer trust against a trust list, and explicitly separate 'missing' from 'invalid'. What it cannot do, in this site's view: guarantee that content is not misleading - an honest edit history can still accompany a deceptive narrative; the official pages scope verification to signatures, bindings and assertions. One often-missed detail: identity assertions are bound separately from signature validation, so 'who signed it' and 'who they claim to be' are two different findings (docs hub on record).
