Langfuse 是开源的观测与评测平台。 它的核心差异是自托管：trace、评分、提示词和数据集都能留在自己的环境里。

Langfuse 是开源的观测与评测平台。 它的核心差异是自托管：trace、评分、提示词和数据集都能留在自己的环境里。

适合与不适合When to use it, when not to

适合数据不能出域、又要「线上看得见的东西变成发布前挡得住的回归」的团队。 它把观测与评测接在同一套 trace 上：先看、再沉淀成数据集、再在 CI 里挡住退化。 不适合只想用厂商侧托管红队或合规审计的场景，也不适合不愿自备 judge 模型密钥的团队。 本站只记它支持哪些评测面，不记它测出来的分数。

模型与开放条件Model & openness

平台自己不产内容，评测量靠你配的模型——LLM-as-a-Judge 走「LLM Connection」， 凭据由用户自配（judge 文档当日直抓在册）；被观测的应用可接任意框架与模型， 自托管另有「Bring your own LLM API」口径（self-hosting 页当日在册）。 judge 可用模型全清单与版本映射本次未逐条清点，记为未知。

The platform produces no content itself; the evaluation capacity depends on models you configure — LLM-as-a-Judge runs through an "LLM Connection" with credentials you supply (judge doc direct-fetched today). Observed apps can use any framework or model, and self-hosting carries a "Bring your own LLM API" line (self-hosting page fetched today). The full judge-model list and version mapping were not itemized today — recorded as unknown.

运行位置Where it runs

开源可自部署（Docker Compose 起测试、Kubernetes/Helm 起生产，self-hosting 页当日在册）， 另有官方云（AWS 美/欧/日三区，security 页当日在册）；交互面是 Web 控制台加 SDK 与 API。 本机跑的是平台服务与界面，不在本机跑被评测的模型。

Open source and self-deployable (Docker Compose for testing, Kubernetes/Helm for production, per the self-hosting page fetched today), plus an official cloud (AWS US/EU/JP regions, security page fetched today); the interaction surface is a web console with SDKs and an API. What runs on your machine is the platform service and UI, not the models under evaluation.

When self-hosted, traces, scores, prompts and datasets live in your own PostgreSQL, ClickHouse, Redis and object storage (fetched today); data arrives via SDK, OpenTelemetry and the API — the platform does not read your local codebase; the export and deletion interface surface was not itemized today.

Tier and quota facts (Hobby plus Core, Pro, Enterprise; usage units and data-access windows vary by tier) are on the pricing page from today, with price numbers excluded per D9. 适合什么任务Best for

适合要把数据留在自有环境（自托管或气隙部署）、观测与评测共用同一套 trace、 把线上样本沉淀成数据集再在 CI 挡回归的团队。不适合只想用厂商侧托管红队或合规审计、 不愿自备 judge 模型密钥的场景；本站不测分也不排名。

Fits teams that must keep data in their own environment (self-hosted or air-gapped), want observation and evaluation on one trace store, and turn production samples into datasets that block regressions in CI. Not a fit for those who only want a vendor-hosted red-team or compliance-audit service, or who will not bring judge-model credentials. This site does not measure scores and does not rank.

  • /ee 边界：核心 MIT 之外，哪些治理与评测能力只存在于企业目录

This site has not completed measured runs; conclusions cover the capability and license surfaces only.

Suggested measurements (three, each pointing at what the readable official pages do not give):

  • Online-evaluation latency and missed coverage: for the same batch of production traces, how long the async judge takes and what the sampling rule is
  • The self-hosting upgrade path: migration cost for ClickHouse and object storage when moving from Docker Compose to Helm
  • The /ee boundary: which governance and evaluation capabilities exist only in the enterprise directory
