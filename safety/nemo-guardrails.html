适合什么任务Best for

适合：要在自己的进程或容器里加可编程护栏——话题控制、PII、越狱检测、工具调用管控， 并且要能换模型与端点。不适合：只想要一个托管控制台带审核工作流、要把评测平台当主轴、 或不能接受「护栏质量取决于所选模型」这件事。

Fits: teams adding programmable rails inside their own process or container - topic control, PII, jailbreak detection, tool-call governance - while keeping the freedom to swap models and endpoints. Not a fit: anyone wanting a hosted console with review workflows, or treating rail quality as a vendor guarantee, since the judgement inherits whichever model runs it.

Suggested measurements (three):

  • How the same rail set behaves across providers (local endpoint versus third-party API)
  • The real consequences of tool messages bypassing input validation, versus the cost of adding output rails
  • What data actually leaves your environment when a third-party guardrail service is attached
