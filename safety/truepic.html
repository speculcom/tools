适合什么任务Best for

适合保险理赔、贷款与抵押、设备勘验这类要「拍摄即验真、事后可举证」的流程，以及要把验真结论接进自有系统的 API 用户。 不适合要 LLM 输入输出过滤的团队（不是它的产品主轴），也不适合要自托管或要免费自助额度的人。

Fits: insurance claims, lending and collateral, equipment inspection - any workflow needing authenticated-at-capture evidence plus a defensible record - and teams wiring authentication verdicts into their own systems through the API. Not a fit: teams looking for LLM input/output filtering (not the product's main axis), anyone needing self-hosting, or anyone expecting a free self-serve quota (the pricing page records no free plan).

能力边界Capability boundaries

在册能判的：媒体是否为签名设备当场采集、是否翻拍屏幕、时间与位置是否自洽、 元数据是否被改动，以及可选的 AI 分析任务。不在册的：它不判内容是否有害， 也不替你决定要不要采信这张图。C2PA 侧的边界是本站观点：凭证给的是来源与处理史， 不是「真实」的终极判定（详见 Content Credentials）。

On record it can judge: whether media was captured live on a signing device, whether a screen was re-photographed, whether time and location are self-consistent, whether metadata was altered, plus optional AI analysis tasks. Not on record: it does not decide whether content is harmful, and it does not decide whether you should trust the picture. The C2PA boundary, in this site's view: credentials give provenance and processing history, not a final verdict on truthfulness (see the Content Credentials entry).
