开源 MIT GGUF C++ 本地推理 llama-server partial lifecycle: active 核验Checked 2026-10-21

llama.cpp 是整个本地推理生态的地基：GGUF 格式由它定义，models 站的量化索引按它的口径建， Ollama/LM Studio/KoboldCpp 都在它上面盖楼。MIT、纯 C/C++、llama-server 提供官方 REST 线。

llama.cpp is the bedrock of the entire local-inference ecosystem: GGUF is defined here, the models-site quant index is built to its yardstick, and Ollama/LM Studio/KoboldCpp all build on top of it. MIT, pure C/C++, with an official REST line via llama-server.

适合与不适合When to use it, when not to

适合：要控制量化档/后端/每个参数的用户、消费级 GPU 与 Apple 硅跑 GGUF、给自研工具做推理底座。 不适合：要开箱 GUI；要多模型高并发服务化（那是 vLLM/SGLang 的调度强项）。

Fits: fine-grained control (quant tiers/backends/params), GGUF on consumer GPUs and Apple silicon, powering in-house tools. Not a fit: out-of-the-box GUIs; multi-model high-concurrency serving.
