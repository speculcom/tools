模型与开放条件Model & openness

由 Canva 的生成式设计能力与第三方模型共同驱动，具体模型不逐项公开。可用功能随套餐与地区变化。

Driven by Canva’s generative design capabilities together with third-party models; the specific models are not disclosed item by item, and available features vary by tier and region.

运行位置Where it runs

运行在 Canva 云端，网页、桌面和移动端都是入口。生成与渲染在服务端完成。

Runs in Canva’s cloud; web, desktop and mobile are all entry points, and generation and rendering happen server-side.

本地文件Local files

可上传图片、字体、品牌素材和文档。本地文件需先上传，不能直接扫描本机目录。

Can upload images, fonts, brand assets and documents; local files must be uploaded and cannot be scanned directly from the local disk.

关机后的任务Tasks after shutdown

设计生成、批量调整在会话内完成。没有关机后持续运行的后台任务。按任务触发执行，结束后不保留常驻代理。长任务需拆分为多次可复核的步骤。

Design generation and bulk adjustments happen within a session; there are no background jobs that survive shutdown. Tasks are triggered per request and no resident agent remains; long work must be split into reviewable steps.

工具与扩展Tools & extensions

与 Canva 编辑器、品牌套件、模板库、Magic Write / Magic Design / 图像编辑集成。可导出多种格式。

Integrated with the Canva editor, Brand Kit, template library, Magic Write / Magic Design and image editing; exports in multiple formats.
