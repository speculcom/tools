CodeBuddy 是腾讯云的三端编码助手（插件/AI IDE/CLI）：混元为主、可接其它大模型， Figma 到代码、CloudStudio 一键部署一条龙；所用混元模型备案号由官方文档公示。

CodeBuddy is Tencent Cloud's three-surface coding assistant (plugin / AI IDE / CLI): Hunyuan as the anchor with other LLMs pluggable, Figma-to-code and one-click CloudStudio deploys; the Hunyuan model's filing number is published in the official docs.

适合与不适合When to use it, when not to

适合：国内团队合规叙事、腾讯云生态建站部署、三端同账号切换。 不适合：要全球模型清单自选、深度依赖海外 SaaS 链。

Fits: mainland teams wanting the compliance narrative (Hunyuan is filed), build-to-deploy inside the Tencent Cloud ecosystem (CloudBase/CloudStudio), switching across plugin+IDE+CLI on one account. Not a fit: self-selecting a global model list; deep dependence on overseas SaaS integration chains.
