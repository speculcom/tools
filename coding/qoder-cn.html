Qoder CN is Alibaba Cloud's AI coding product line (formerly Lingma, officially renamed 2026-05-20): desktop IDE + JetBrains plugin + CLI, switchable mainstream domestic models or BYOK, three context layers (memory / Repo Wiki / knowledge base), Credits shared across the family — 'first-party Alibaba Cloud product + domestic deployment' is its official compliance narrative.
