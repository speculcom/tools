文心快码（Baidu Comate）是百度的 AI 编码全家桶：Comate AI IDE + VS Code/JetBrains/Visual Studio/Xcode 插件 + CLI，官方模型页以国产模型为主（DeepSeek/GLM/Kimi/MiniMax 可切、企业可加千帆）， Skills/Rules/Subagents/MCP/Memory/Automation 扩展面全成页，个人/企业/私有化三档—— 私有化「数据不出域」是它的官方合规主打。

Baidu Comate (Wenxin Kuaima) is Baidu's AI coding family: a standalone Comate AI IDE + plugins for VS Code/JetBrains/Visual Studio/Xcode + CLI, with a model menu dominated by domestic LLMs (DeepSeek/GLM/Kimi/MiniMax switchable, enterprises can add Qianfan providers), a full extension stack (Skills/Rules/Subagents/MCP/Memory/Automation) and personal/enterprise/private-deployment tiers — 'data stays in-domain' private deployment is its official compliance flagship.

适合与不适合When to use it, when not to

适合：国内合规与私有化叙事（金融/医疗客群官方点名）、IDE 覆盖广（含 Xcode/Visual Studio）、企业控制台与 AgentHub 资产中台。 不适合：要海外前沿模型清单、要个人 BYOK 接任意自有 Key（未核验到）。

Fits: domestic-compliance and private-deployment narratives (finance/healthcare named by the official pages), broad IDE coverage (including Xcode/Visual Studio), enterprise console + AgentHub asset hub. Not a fit: wanting an overseas frontier-model menu (the official model page is domestic-first); wanting personal BYOK with arbitrary keys (enterprise adds Qianfan/third-party providers; personal BYOK not verified).
