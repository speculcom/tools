练习包 · Creator：把一份官方资料做成三件套并留下事实表Exercise: three assets from one official source

规划Planning

选一份官方产品页，产出短文、图像提示词与 30 秒口播稿，并附 5 条事实核对表与 2 条被拒的说法；评分标准看引用、边界与一致性。

Take one official product page and produce a short post, an image prompt and a 30-second script, plus a five-claim fact table and two rejected claims; the rubric checks citations, boundaries and consistency.

任务The task

选一份有发布日期的官方产品页或 changelog，做一组可发布的三件套。只准用这一份来源，不补二手总结。篇幅和评分线是本练习约束；口播时长需实际朗读确认。

  1. 短文：150–200 字，说清「变了什么、影响谁、要做什么」，标题不夸张。
  2. 图像提示词：一段可复现的英文提示词，描述一个具体场景，不生成商标或真人肖像。
  3. 30 秒口播稿：约 90–110 字，按「变化 → 影响 → 动作」三段写。

同时交一张事实表：5 条关键说法，每条写来源句子与日期；再列 2 条你决定不写的说法及原因。

Choose an official product page or changelog with a publication date and create three assets ready for review before publication. Use that one source only, without secondary summaries. Lengths and passing scores are exercise constraints; confirm speaking duration by reading the script aloud.

  1. Short post: 150–200 Chinese characters explaining what changed, who is affected and what to do, without an exaggerated headline.
  2. Image prompt: an English prompt describing one specific scene, without generating trademarks or real people's likenesses.
  3. Thirty-second script: approximately 90–110 Chinese characters, organised as change → impact → action.

Also supply a fact table with five key claims, a source sentence and date for each, plus two claims you decided not to include and why.

参考答案结构Model answer outline

事实表的合格行：

说法：[来源中的具体版本] 于 [发布日期] 发布
来源句：[复制来源中的原句，不凭记忆补写]
日期：[来源标注的发布日期]

被拒说法的合格写法：

不写：「这个版本让所有浏览器 Agent 都变快了」
原因：来源只列了配置与功能变更，没有性能对比数据；跨产品结论无证据。

口播稿的合格结构：

变化：[具体版本新增或移除了什么，附来源]
影响：[哪些工作流受到影响，未确认的收益不写]
动作：[需要检查的配置或迁移步骤]

An acceptable fact-table row:

Claim: [specific version from source] was released on [publication date].
Source sentence: [copy the actual sentence, not a reconstruction from memory].
Date: [publication date stated by the source].

An acceptable rejected claim:

Omit: “This release makes every browser agent faster.”
Reason: The source lists configuration and feature changes, not comparative performance data.
It does not support a cross-product conclusion.

An acceptable script structure:

Change: [what this version added or removed, with source].
Impact: [which workflows are affected; omit unconfirmed benefits].
Action: [configuration checks or migration steps].

常见失分Common misses

  • 把官方宣传句直接当事实：宣传语不是可核事实。
  • 三件套各写一套数字：读者对不上。
  • 图像提示词堆形容词：没有具体场景，生成结果不可控。
  • 拒绝说法写成「感觉不对」：要指向证据缺口或适用边界。
  • An official promotional claim is not automatically a verified fact.
  • Different numbers across the three assets prevent readers from reconciling them.
  • A pile of adjectives does not specify a concrete image scene.
  • “It feels wrong” is not a reason to reject a claim; identify missing evidence or scope limits.
