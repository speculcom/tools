逐项适用条件When each fits

ChatGPT：适合要通用问答、文件分析、图像生成与智能体都在一个入口的人；Deep Research、智能体与图像 2.0 已并入主产品，不用再找独立入口。能力边界：浏览器 Atlas 已停运，浏览器操作能力改走 ChatGPT / Codex 线。什么情况下选另一个：要 Google 搜索与 Workspace 联动，选 Gemini；要长文写作与可逐条编辑的记忆，选 Claude。

Gemini：适合已在 Google 生态、要搜索与 Workspace 联动的人；研究线有 Deep Research 与 Deep Research Max，图像线有 Nano Banana 2。数据条款的关键是 Keep Activity：关闭后未来对话不用于训练，临时聊天保留 72 小时且不训练。可达性记录：本站探测环境在 2026-10-20 两次请求未取得 HTTP 状态码（000），不能代表所有地区与网络；需在你的环境中确认。什么情况下选另一个：需要大陆直连，选国产助手；要终端编码 agent，另看 Gemini CLI。

Claude：适合长文档分析、写作与内容产出；记忆是可逐条编辑的清单，Cowork 把「关机续跑、定时任务」写进官方 release notes。数据条款提供训练 opt-out，安全审查是官方明写的例外。什么情况下选另一个：要免费联网问答与 Windows 内嵌入口，选 Copilot；要图像生成线，选 ChatGPT 或 Gemini。

Microsoft Copilot：适合 Windows / Edge / Bing 生态用户与已在微软企业体系的人；免费账号即可用联网问答。能力边界：C 端 Deep Research 已于 2026-08-18 退役，符合条件的 Microsoft 365 Premium 订阅可用 Researcher；模型由微软路由，选择面有限。什么情况下选另一个：要自选模型或跨平台深度研究，选 ChatGPT / Gemini。

ChatGPT: a candidate for general questions, file analysis, image generation and agent workflows through one product. The snapshot places Deep Research, agents and Images 2.0 in the main product, rather than separate entry points. Atlas is retired in that record; browser operation follows ChatGPT / Codex. Consider Gemini for Google Search and Workspace integration, or Claude for document writing and individually editable memory.

Gemini: a candidate for Google ecosystem users who need Search and Workspace integration. The recorded research lines are Deep Research and Deep Research Max, with Nano Banana 2 for images. The snapshot describes Keep Activity: future chats are not used for training after it is disabled; temporary chats are retained for 72 hours without training. Access record: two requests from this site's probe environment on 2026-10-20 returned no HTTP status (000). That does not represent every region or network; check your own environment. Consider a domestic assistant where direct domestic access is required, or Gemini CLI for terminal coding tasks.

Claude: a candidate for long-document analysis, writing and content production. The snapshot describes individually editable memory and Cowork continuation/scheduling. Its training opt-out has an explicitly documented safety-review exception. Consider Copilot for free web-connected questions and Windows integration, or ChatGPT / Gemini for image-generation workflows.

Microsoft Copilot: a candidate for Windows / Edge / Bing users and Microsoft enterprise environments, with web-connected questions for free accounts in this snapshot. Consumer Deep Research was retired on 2026-08-18; the research path described here is Researcher for eligible Microsoft 365 Premium subscribers. Microsoft routes models, with limited user choice. Examine ChatGPT / Gemini for other model-choice or cross-platform research requirements.

价格档位怎么看Pricing tiers

不报数字，只给读法：

  1. 看计费单位：按席位还是按用量。按席位费用需乘实际席位数，再与用量方案按同一期间比较。
  2. 看免费档缺什么：长上下文、文件上传、深度研究、图像生成、团队权限通常分层。
  3. 看导出：聊天记录与生成内容能否导出；缺少导出会增加迁移限制；还需确认格式与导出后的可用性。
  4. 看企业线：Team / Enterprise 的默认训练策略、权限与审计与消费档不同，别拿消费条款套企业。

四个产品的档位名与配额以官方定价页为准（链接见 sources）。

Read the billing structure rather than a price quotation here:

  1. Billing unit: seats or usage. Multiply seat rates by actual seat count and compare over the same period.
  2. Free-tier exclusions: inspect context, file uploads, research, image generation and team permissions separately.
  3. Export: check whether conversations and generated content can be exported. Missing export increases migration constraints; verify formats and usability afterwards too.
  4. Enterprise terms: Team / Enterprise training defaults, permissions and audits can differ from consumer tiers. Do not apply consumer terms to enterprise use.

Use official pricing pages for current tier names and quotas; source references are below.

组合建议A workable pairing

下面是按功能与生态分工的候选组合，不是效果排名；仍需按上方约束筛选。

  • 中文与国内直连为主：国产助手做主，ChatGPT / Gemini 做备选（需要稳定网络路径）。
  • 研究与写作：Claude 主写作，Gemini 主研究线，ChatGPT 主图像与智能体。
  • Windows 企业：Copilot 做入口，M365 Researcher 做深度研究，ChatGPT 做通用补充。
  • 只选一个：先按数据条款与大陆可达排除，再按研究线选；不要按榜单分数选。

These are candidate combinations organised by features and ecosystem, not measured quality rankings. Apply the constraints above first.

  • Chinese and direct domestic access: consider a domestic assistant as the primary, with ChatGPT / Gemini as alternatives where network access is available.
  • Research and writing: consider Claude for writing, Gemini for the research workflow, and ChatGPT for images and agent tasks.
  • Windows enterprise: consider Copilot as an entry point, M365 Researcher for research, and ChatGPT for other general tasks.
  • One assistant only: filter by data terms and access, then by the research workflow you need; do not choose from an aggregate leaderboard.

常见误判Common misreads

  • 把「能关训练」当成「默认不训练」：需要分别核对默认值、开关与例外，不能跨产品或档位推断。
  • 把「API 条款」套到消费应用：Gemini Apps 隐私声明与 Gemini API 附加条款是两份文件。
  • 把「浏览器停运」当成「能力消失」：Atlas 停运后能力并入 ChatGPT / Codex，入口变了。
  • 把「C 端 Copilot」与「M365 Copilot」混为一谈：两个产品面，条款与能力不同。
  • An available training opt-out is not the same as training being off by default. Check defaults, controls and exceptions for each product and tier.
  • API terms and consumer-application terms are separate: Gemini Apps privacy notices and Gemini API additional terms are different documents.
  • Retirement of a browser does not necessarily remove its capabilities; the recorded Atlas transition moved entry points into ChatGPT / Codex.
  • Consumer Copilot and M365 Copilot are different product surfaces with different terms and capabilities.
