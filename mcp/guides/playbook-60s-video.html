任务：做一条 60 秒产品视频Playbook: producing a 60-second product video

规划Planning

脚本、分镜、生成、剪辑、权利登记五段；含单段时长限制、成本算式、失败模式与交付清单。

Five stages — script, storyboard, generation, editing and rights logging — with per-clip limits, a cost formula, failure modes and a delivery checklist.

准备：先写能拍的分镜Prepare: a shootable storyboard

下面的镜头数、3–8 秒时长及横竖两版是本例的交付安排，不是所有视频的通用规范；按发布渠道、内容与模型限制调整。

  1. 一句话卖点：60 秒只讲一件事，写不出就砍。
  2. 分镜表：镜号、时长、画面、字幕、音效，每镜 3–8 秒。
  3. 素材清单：产品图、Logo、参考视频、品牌色与字体授权。
  4. 权利预检：音乐、字体、素材图、数字人肖像，逐项记来源与许可。

逐镜检查信息是否重复、主体是否一致；镜头多时可以先合并，但不设统一镜头上限。

Shot counts, three-to-eight-second durations and two aspect-ratio deliverables are example choices, not universal production rules. Adjust to channel, content and model limits.

  1. One core message for the 60-second piece.
  2. A shot table: ID, duration, visual, subtitles and sound; the example uses 3–8 seconds per shot.
  3. An asset inventory: product images, logo, references, brand colours and font rights.
  4. A rights precheck for music, fonts, images and likenesses, with provenance and permission records.

Inspect repetition and subject consistency shot by shot. Merge where useful; there is no universal shot-count ceiling.

生成：按模型限制切段Generate within clip limits

  • Kling 4.0 单次原生 30 秒，60 秒片至少切两段（官方站，2026-10 快照）。
  • 需要多素材参考时，Kling 的全能参考支持 15 项素材；超出就分组。
  • 每段单独保存 prompt、种子（若有）、模型版本与生成日期。
  • 如果所选模型支持首尾帧控制，可按对应文档尝试，并检查连接效果；不假定所有模型都支持。

失败模式：同段场景和动作过多时，可能出现主体或动作不一致；先简化为一个明确动作，再检查结果。

  • The recorded Kling 4.0 limit is native 30-second generation, requiring at least two such segments for 60 seconds (official site; October 2026 snapshot).
  • Its recorded reference feature accepts up to 15 assets; group inputs when needed.
  • Save prompts, seeds if available, model versions and generation dates per clip.
  • If the selected model supports first/last-frame control, follow its documentation and inspect the join; do not assume universal support.

Failure mode: too many scenes/actions can produce inconsistent subjects or motion. Simplify to one clear action and check the result.

剪辑：先粗剪再精修Edit: rough cut first

  1. 按分镜顺序拼段，先不看转场。
  2. 统一色调与画幅；不同模型出来的色偏要手动校。
  3. 加字幕与品牌元素；字幕字号按移动端可读性定。
  4. 配乐与音效：只用已登记许可的音源。
  5. 输出两版：16:9 主版与 9:16 竖版。

决策点：生成素材不够用时，补拍产品实拍，不要用生成模型硬凑产品细节。

  1. Assemble shots in storyboard order before polishing transitions.
  2. Match colour and aspect ratio, correcting differences between sources.
  3. Add subtitles and brand elements, checking readability on a phone.
  4. Use music and sound with recorded permissions.
  5. Produce the example's 16:9 and 9:16 versions.

If generated footage does not accurately represent the product, obtain suitable real footage instead of inventing product details.

成本算式Cost formula

生成成本 = Σ 每镜头的 (计费秒数 × 每秒单价 × 总尝试次数)
总成本 ≈ 生成 + 剪辑人力 + 音乐/字体许可 + 返工

上式用于按秒计费，总尝试次数包含第一次；按积分或请求计费时先换算单位。尝试预算按自己的假设或历史记录填写，不预设 2–3 次。用 成本计算器 复算。

Generation cost = sum over shots of
  (billed seconds × rate per second × total attempts)
Total cost ≈ generation + editing labour + music/font licences + rework

This applies to per-second billing, with the first attempt included. Convert credit/request billing to the appropriate unit. Use your own assumptions or records for attempts, not a default two or three. Recalculate with the cost calculator.

验收单Acceptance checklist

  • [ ] 60 秒成片，卖点只讲一件事
  • [ ] 每段生成参数与模型版本已存档
  • [ ] 字幕在手机屏可读
  • [ ] 音乐、字体、素材、数字人四项许可已登记
  • [ ] 16:9 与 9:16 两版输出
  • [ ] 需要溯源标注的平台已标注
  • [ ] 权利表与成片一起归档
  • [ ] Sixty-second deliverable with one core message.
  • [ ] Generation settings and model versions retained per clip.
  • [ ] Subtitles readable on a phone.
  • [ ] Music, font, image and likeness permissions recorded.
  • [ ] The example's 16:9 and 9:16 versions exported.
  • [ ] Required provenance/disclosure labels included.
  • [ ] Rights log archived with the finished video.

常见失败模式Failure modes

  • 镜头过密：60 秒塞 20 个镜头，观众记不住卖点。
  • 主体漂移：同段多主体，模型把产品变形。
  • 许可漏登：音乐换了三首，只记了最后一首。
  • 版本不可追：没记模型版本，返工时无法复现。
  • 竖版后补：先做 16:9 再裁 9:16，主体被切掉。
  • Dense editing: too many shots distract from the intended message.
  • Subject drift: multiple subjects/actions yield inconsistent product appearance.
  • Incomplete rights records: only the final music selection is logged.
  • Missing versions: rework cannot trace the original model/settings.
  • Late vertical framing: cropping the horizontal version cuts off the subject.

贯穿案例：一分钟活动介绍Worked example: a one-minute event introduction

假设任务是介绍一次旧书交换，已有确认的时间地点与可用素材。这里是制作计划，不是已生成的视频。

先分三段：0–10 秒说明活动；10–45 秒展示交换流程；45–60 秒给出时间地点。时长只是本例安排，按实际内容调整。

用同一份已确认资料写旁白和字幕。每个镜头记录素材来源、许可与需要补的内容；没有真实画面时，可以使用明确标注的示意图，避免让人误以为拍摄过活动。

合成后分别检查：信息是否一致、字幕是否能读完、配音是否压过背景声、画面是否误导、发布平台是否要求生成内容标识。总时长合格不代表这些条件都已满足。

Suppose the task introduces a book exchange with confirmed time, venue and permitted assets. This is a production plan, not a generated video.

Plan three parts: 0–10 seconds for the event, 10–45 for the exchange process, 45–60 for time/venue. These are example timings, adjustable to the material.

Use one confirmed source brief for narration and subtitles. Record each shot's provenance, permission and missing material. Clearly label illustrative images so they do not imply footage of an event that was never filmed.

Check factual consistency, subtitle reading time, audio balance, misleading visuals and platform labeling requirements. Meeting the duration alone does not establish acceptance.
