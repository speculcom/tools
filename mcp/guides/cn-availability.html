国内可用性怎么读：直连、主体与备案的口径How to read mainland availability

护栏与安全Guardrails & safety

用 tools 档案里的 cn 三个字段回答「国内能不能连、谁在运营、有没有备案」；给出 225 份档案的统计口径、限制与自查方法，不做实时可用性承诺。

Three cn fields in the tool dossiers answer whether a product connects from the mainland, who operates it and whether it is filed; this page gives the counting rules, limits and a self-check method for 225 dossiers, with no real-time guarantee.

先说这不是什么What this page is not

这页解释字段怎么读，不是可用性数据库。

  • 不承诺「现在能连」：直连结果是某个日期、某条网络路径的一次实测，网络和产品都会变。
  • 不替你判断合规：备案字段只记录官方公示里有没有这个名字，不构成法律意见。
  • 不排名：字段是事实记录，不是推荐顺序。

This page explains how to read fields; it is not a live availability database.

  • A direct-access result records a date and network path, not a guarantee that access works now.
  • Filing fields record evidence found in official notices, not a legal determination about your deployment.
  • Fields are factual records, not a recommendation ranking.

三个字段Three fields

每份 tools 档案末尾都有一段 cn:，对外只暴露三个判断：

字段取值回答的问题怎么用
origindomestic / foreign运营主体在境内还是境外仅作主体线索，不直接推断数据驻留或法律义务
directyes / no / unknown从大陆网络能否直连官网决定要不要额外网络方案；unknown 就是没测到
filingfiled / not-filed / unknown官方备案公示里有没有记录只对面向境内公众提供服务的产品有意义

字段旁边通常还有 direct_note、filing_note、verified，记录实测日期、被测域名与公示批次。读结论前先读备注。

Each tool dossier contains a cn: block with three classifications:

FieldValuesQuestionUse
origindomestic / foreignWhere is the operating entity based?An entity clue, not proof of data residency or legal obligations
directyes / no / unknownWas the official site reachable directly from the recorded mainland network?Assess your network requirements; unknown means not confirmed
filingfiled / not-filed / unknownWas a matching filing recorded?Relevant context for services offered to the mainland public

Read accompanying direct_note, filing_note and verified values for probe dates, domains and notice batches before using the classification.

直连实测的口径How direct was measured

direct 不是 ping，也不是「我朋友说能打开」。档案里的口径是：

  1. 选产品主域名与关键页（官网、条款、定价、文档）。
  2. 从固定的境内网络发起请求，记录状态码与时间。
  3. 200 记 yes；超时、DNS 失败、连接重置记 no；只有探测环境限制（如反爬 403）记 unknown。
  4. 记录日期。同一产品换网络、换省份可能不同。

所以 direct: yes 的准确读法是「在该次实测的网络路径上可达」，不是「全国所有网络都可达」。

direct is not a ping result or someone else's anecdote. The dossier convention is:

  1. Choose the main product domain and key official, terms, pricing and documentation pages.
  2. Request them from the recorded mainland network, retaining status and timing.
  3. Record 200 as yes; timeout, DNS failure or reset as no; probe-environment restrictions such as anti-bot 403 as unknown.
  4. Record the date. Networks and provinces may give different results.

Thus yes means reachable on that recorded path, not on every network nationwide.

备案字段怎么读Reading the filing field

  • filed：官方公示里有主体与产品名，档案记录批次、备案号与日期。
  • not-filed：档案在所查公示范围内未记录匹配项。可能涉及产品名、主体名、批次、登记类型或适用范围差异，不能直接解释成未履行义务。
  • unknown：没查到或公示口径对不上，不做推断。

《生成式人工智能服务管理暂行办法》第二条以向境内公众提供相关服务界定适用范围，第十七条另规定具有舆论属性或社会动员能力服务的安全评估与算法备案要求。不能只按厂商注册地判断；还需区分服务备案、应用登记与其他义务。备案字段不证明技术可用、数据驻留或全部合规要求已满足。

  • filed: the dossier records a matching entity/product in an official notice, with batch, number and date.
  • not-filed: no match was recorded within the notices searched. Product/entity names, batches, registration types or scope may differ; this does not directly establish non-compliance.
  • unknown: insufficient evidence or unmatched notice scope; do not infer an answer.

Article 2 of the Interim Measures scopes relevant services offered to the mainland public. Article 17 separately addresses security assessment and algorithm filing for services with public-opinion attributes or social-mobilisation capacity. Entity location alone does not decide applicability. Distinguish service filing, application registration and other obligations. A filing field does not prove availability, residency or complete compliance.

怎么自查一个产品Self-check in four steps

  1. 打开该产品的 tools 档案，读 cn 三个字段与 verified 日期。
  2. 看日期：超过 90 天就当作过期线索，不要当现状。
  3. 自己复测一次主域名：记录日期、网络、状态码；与档案不一致时以你的实测为准，并记下差异。
  4. 涉及上线：把「主体 / 直连 / 备案」三行写进选型表，再单独列数据驻留与合同主体。
  1. Open the tool dossier and read the three cn fields and verification date.
  2. Treat information older than 90 days as a lead needing rechecking, not current status.
  3. Repeat the reachability check yourself, recording date, network and HTTP status. Retain differences from the dossier.
  4. For deployment, record entity, access and filing separately, alongside data residency and contracting entity.

常见误读Common misreads

  • 把 not-filed 直接当违规，或把境外主体直接当作义务不适用：都超出了字段能证明的范围。
  • 把 direct: yes 当 SLA：那是一次实测，不是服务承诺。
  • 把 origin: domestic 当数据一定境内：主体在境内不等于所有数据都留在境内，仍要看隐私政策与部署方式。
  • 把 unknown 当 no：没测到与不可达是两件事。
  • 拿旧日期当今天：字段带核验日，过期就要复测。
  • Neither not-filed nor foreign origin alone establishes a violation or exemption; those conclusions exceed the fields' evidence.
  • Direct access yes is not an SLA; it is one recorded observation.
  • A domestic entity does not prove all data remains domestic; examine privacy terms and deployment.
  • Unknown and no are different.
  • Old check dates are not today's observations.

核验事实Dated facts

  1. 225 份档案均带 cn 块；统计为境内 37 / 境外 188（2026-11-03 汇总）。
  2. 直连实测分布为可以 169 / 不可以 46 / 未测到 10（同上）。
  3. 备案分布为已备案 20 / 未备案 190 / 未知 15（同上）。

  1. All 225 dossiers contain a cn block: 37 domestic and 188 foreign (recorded aggregation 2026-11-03).
  2. Stored direct-access counts are 169 yes, 46 no and 10 unknown.
  3. Stored filing counts are 20 filed, 190 not-filed and 15 unknown.
  4. The CAC notice dated 2024-04-02 has rolling attachments, not just its initial batch. On 2026-11-04, the notice, Articles 2 and 17 of the Interim Measures and this page's aggregate counts were rechecked. Individual products' filing and network statuses were not all rechecked.
