这一篇讲什么What this guide covers

图像、视频、音频工具的第一道筛子不是「效果好不好」，而是商用许可。 第二道是输入输出与编辑链，第三道是本地还是云端，最后才是价格。 本篇不排名、不报价格；许可原文与定价以官方页为准。

<!-- EN -->

The first screen for image, video and audio tools is not "does it look good" but commercial licensing. The second is input/output and edit chains, the third is local vs cloud, and price comes last. This guide ranks nothing and quotes no prices; licence text and pricing follow the official pages.

第一道：商用许可First: commercial licensing

第二道：输入输出与编辑链Second: input/output and edit chain

  • 输入：文生图、图生图、视频生视频、音频驱动口型，支持的输入类型决定工作流。
  • 输出：分辨率、时长、水印、文件格式；能否导出无损或分层文件。
  • 编辑链：能否局部重绘、扩图、替换主体、保持角色一致性；是否支持时间线或分镜。
  • 批量与 API：要量产就看是否有官方 API、并发限制与商用条款，而不是只看网页版。

<!-- EN -->

- Input: text-to-image, image-to-image, video-to-video, audio-driven lip sync; supported inputs determine the workflow. - Output: resolution, duration, watermark, file format; whether lossless or layered files can be exported. - Edit chain: inpainting, outpainting, subject replacement, character consistency; timeline or storyboard support. - Batch and API: for volume, check official APIs, concurrency limits and commercial terms, not just the web UI.
  • Input: text-to-image, image-to-image, video-to-video, audio-driven lip sync; supported inputs determine the workflow.
  • Output: resolution, duration, watermark, file format; whether lossless or layered files can be exported.
  • Edit chain: inpainting, outpainting, subject replacement, character consistency; timeline or storyboard support.
  • Batch and API: for volume, check official APIs, concurrency limits and commercial terms, not just the web UI.

第三道：本地还是云端Third: local vs cloud

  • 本地：模型权重与算力在你手里，适合隐私敏感、离线、长期高频；代价是显卡、部署与运维。
  • 云端：开箱即用、模型更新快；代价是按量计费、数据出境与网络依赖。
  • 混合：本地跑批量、云端做难例，是常见折中；要确认两端产出的格式与许可一致。

<!-- EN -->

- Local: weights and compute stay with you — good for privacy, offline use and high long-term volume; the cost is GPUs, deployment and operations. - Cloud: ready to use and fast to update; the cost is metered billing, cross-border data and network dependency. - Hybrid: local for batches, cloud for hard cases — a common compromise; confirm both sides produce compatible formats and licences.
  • Local: weights and compute stay with you — good for privacy, offline use and high long-term volume; the cost is GPUs, deployment and operations.
  • Cloud: ready to use and fast to update; the cost is metered billing, cross-border data and network dependency.
  • Hybrid: local for batches, cloud for hard cases — a common compromise; confirm both sides produce compatible formats and licences.

第四道：中文与区域可用Fourth: Chinese and regional access

  • 中文提示词的遵循度、中文文字渲染、中文语音的自然度，都要用你自己的样本测。
  • 国内直连、备案与数据出境是独立约束；海外工具在大陆可能直连超时。
  • 商用发布还要看平台对 AI 生成内容的标识要求（水印、内容凭证）。

<!-- EN -->

- Test Chinese prompt adherence, Chinese text rendering and Chinese speech naturalness with your own samples. - Mainland reachability, filing and cross-border data are separate constraints; overseas tools may time out from the mainland. - Commercial publishing also depends on AI-content labelling rules (watermarks, content credentials).
  • Test Chinese prompt adherence, Chinese text rendering and Chinese speech naturalness with your own samples.
  • Mainland reachability, filing and cross-border data are separate constraints; overseas tools may time out from the mainland.
  • Commercial publishing also depends on AI-content labelling rules (watermarks, content credentials).

常见误判Common misreads

  • 把「免费可用」当成「可商用」：免费档通常不含商用授权。
  • 把「生成效果好」当成「工作流可用」：不能局部修改或保持一致性，产量一大会返工。
  • 把「有网页版」当成「有 API」：量产要看官方 API 与并发限制。
  • 忽略字体与音乐：生成画面之外的字体、配乐、音效仍受各自许可约束。

<!-- EN -->

- Mistaking "free to use" for "licensed for commercial use": free tiers usually exclude commercial rights. - Mistaking "good output" for "usable workflow": without local editing or consistency, volume creates rework. - Mistaking "has a web UI" for "has an API": volume depends on official APIs and concurrency limits. - Ignoring fonts and music: fonts, scores and sound effects outside the generated image keep their own licences.
  • Mistaking "free to use" for "licensed for commercial use": free tiers usually exclude commercial rights.
  • Mistaking "good output" for "usable workflow": without local editing or consistency, volume creates rework.
  • Mistaking "has a web UI" for "has an API": volume depends on official APIs and concurrency limits.
  • Ignoring fonts and music: fonts, scores and sound effects outside the generated image keep their own licences.
