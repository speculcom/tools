这一篇讲什么What this guide covers

选聊天助手不是选「最强模型」，而是先定四个约束：中文、数据、价格、生态。 四个约束会先排除一批候选，剩下的再按场景看。 本篇不排名、不报价格；价格与档位以官方定价页为准。

按场景缩小Narrow by scenario

  • 通用问答与写作：看免费档是否够用、导出是否方便、是否支持文件上传。
  • 长文与研究：看 Deep Research 类功能、引用来源、能否中断补充（ChatGPT 官方 2026-02-10 更新了这些能力）。
  • 中文与国内直连：优先有国内镜像或直连可达的产品；备案与数据出境单独核对。
  • 企业数据：看 Team/Enterprise 线的默认训练策略、权限与审计，而不是消费档。
  • 要浏览器/电脑操作：看 Agent 类功能是否有官方文档与权限边界；Atlas 浏览器已于 2026-08-09 停运，能力并入 ChatGPT/Codex。

免费与付费的边界The free/paid boundary

不报价格，只给判断方法：

  1. 看限额单位：按消息数、按 token、按任务数，还是按席位？单位不同，重度使用者的成本差别很大。
  2. 看能力差：免费档是否缺长上下文、文件上传、深度研究、图像生成或团队权限。
  3. 看导出：聊天记录与生成内容能否导出；不能导出就等于锁定。
  4. 看团队功能：共享空间、权限、审计日志通常只在付费档。

<!-- EN -->

No prices here, only a method: 1. Check the unit: messages, tokens, tasks or seats? The unit changes the cost dramatically for heavy users. 2. Check the capability gap: does the free tier lack long context, file upload, deep research, image generation or team permissions? 3. Check export: can chats and generated content be exported? If not, you are locked in. 4. Check team features: shared spaces, permissions and audit logs usually exist only on paid tiers.

No prices here, only a method:

  1. Check the unit: messages, tokens, tasks or seats? The unit changes the cost dramatically for heavy users.
  2. Check the capability gap: does the free tier lack long context, file upload, deep research, image generation or team permissions?
  3. Check export: can chats and generated content be exported? If not, you are locked in.
  4. Check team features: shared spaces, permissions and audit logs usually exist only on paid tiers.

常见误判Common misreads

  • 把「模型最强」当成「最适合」：中文、数据条款、生态绑定都可能比模型分数更影响日常使用。
  • 把「免费」当成「零成本」：时间、隐私与迁移成本也是成本。
  • 把「能关训练」当成「默认不训练」：多数消费产品默认可能训练，要手动关。
  • 把消费档条款套到企业档：两条线的数据条款与权限不同。

<!-- EN -->

- Mistaking "strongest model" for "best fit": Chinese quality, data terms and ecosystem lock-in often matter more than benchmark scores. - Mistaking "free" for "zero cost": time, privacy and migration costs are costs too. - Mistaking "can opt out" for "does not train by default": most consumer products may train by default and require manual opt-out. - Applying consumer terms to enterprise tiers: the two lines differ in data terms and permissions.
