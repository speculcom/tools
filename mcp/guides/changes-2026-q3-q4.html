怎么用这页How to use this page

区分三种日期：事件发生或计划生效日、来源页面日期、本站核对日。十条记录不是所有产品的完整更新清单。

  • 先核对自己使用的版本、地区与订阅是否受影响。
  • 链接能打开只证明访问成功，不证明全部功能、条款或迁移已核验。
  • 发布日期未核时明确标注，不把当前文档的能力说明反推成首发新闻。
  • 对计划停用事项，先保留需要的数据，再核对目标入口的重建要求。

Distinguish event/effective dates, source-page dates and this site's check date. These ten records are not a complete changelog for every product.

  • Check whether your version, region and subscription are affected.
  • A working link does not establish that all features, terms or migration steps were verified.
  • Mark unconfirmed launch dates rather than inferring them from current documentation.
  • For planned shutdowns, preserve needed data and inspect the destination's rebuilding requirements.
