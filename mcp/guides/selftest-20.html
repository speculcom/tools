怎么用How to use

先只读题目，把答案写下来；做完再对答案。每题 2 分，共 40 分。分数仅定位本练习中的复习需求，不能证明生产部署或带教能力。法规题用于练习识别适用前提，具体事项需回原文确认；对应官方入口于 2026-11-04 局部复核。

  • 36–40：可开始综合练习，用交付物检验理解。
  • 28–35：合格，回看错题对应的站内页。
  • 27 及以下：先按错题主题补基础，再回来重做。

Write your answers before reading the key. Each question earns two points, for a total of forty. Scores identify review needs within this exercise; they do not establish production or teaching competence. Legal questions practise identifying scope rather than deciding individual cases; the linked official references were rechecked on 2026-11-04.

  • 36–40: start an applied exercise and inspect its deliverables.
  • 28–35: review the relevant site pages for missed questions.
  • 27 or below: review the missed topics before repeating the exercise.

题目Questions

  1. RAG 可按哪三个阶段理解？重排是否必需？
  2. 什么情况下不该用 RAG，而应该直接调用模型或改流程？
  3. 文档分块过大和过小各有什么代价？
  4. 混合检索（关键词 + 向量）主要弥补什么缺口？
  5. 重排（rerank）在 RAG 里起什么作用，代价是什么？
  6. 挑 embedding 模型时，除维度外还要看哪三件事？
  7. 「上下文窗口越大越好」为什么是误区？至少说两点。
  8. 「量化会让模型能力减半」为什么是误区？正确说法是什么？
  9. 显存不够就一定跑不了本地模型吗？说明两种可行做法。
  10. 读模型许可证时，哪两个条款决定能不能商用与再分发？
  11. 模型卡里最该先看哪四项？
  12. 公共榜单分数能直接采信吗？说明两个打折理由。
  13. 防提示注入的三条基本做法是什么？
  14. 给 AI 工具配权限的四条原则是什么？
  15. 列出三条常见的 AI 数据泄露路径。
  16. 对照 EU AI Act 自查应记录哪些前提与依据？
  17. 判断国内生成式 AI 备案义务，为什么不能只看注册地？
  18. 换模型前必须核的五件事是什么？
  19. 成本算式里除了 token 单价，还要算哪三类成本？
  20. 验证 AI 输出的四个可操作动作是什么？

参考答案Answer key

  1. 资料准备与索引、检索、基于资料生成并核对引用。重排是可选增强，不是每条 RAG 流程必需。
  2. 知识不在文档里、需要实时查询、权限边界极复杂，或任务本身只需推理与格式转换时，RAG 会增加链路与失败点。
  3. 过大：噪声多、命中不精、浪费上下文；过小：丢上下文、指代断裂、答案碎片化。
  4. 专有名词、编号、错误码等精确匹配；向量检索可能漏掉对应项，关键词检索可补充，仍需评估召回。
  5. 在召回后按相关性重新排序，提高 top-k 精度；代价是额外模型调用与延迟。
  6. 语言覆盖、单位成本与吞吐、在你的领域数据上的自建评测。
  7. 中间信息容易被忽略；长上下文成本更高；塞满不等于理解，检索质量仍决定答案。
  8. 量化损失依任务与位宽而定，4-bit 在很多任务可用；必须自测，不是固定减半。
  9. 不一定；可检查更小模型、更低位宽、CPU/内存 offload 或多设备方案。分别核对后端支持、容量、延迟、通信与任务质量；offload 本身不等于降低权重精度。
  10. 商用范围与再分发/衍生作品条款；「开源」不等于「可商用」。
  11. 训练数据与用途、评测方法与结果、已知限制、版本与日期。
  12. 评测集可能被污染；提示词与任务分布不同；厂商自报口径不统一。
  13. 把外部内容当不可信数据；指令与数据分离；最小权限 + 输出校验。
  14. 最小权限、白名单、关键动作审批、权限可撤销。
  15. 提示词里带敏感信息、检索语料越权、日志与导出、工具调用把数据发到外部。
  16. 记录区域、角色、用途与风险判断，再核对义务、法规版本及适用日期；欧委会提供对应官方入口，不能把旧时间表当作当前规定。
  17. 暂行办法第二条涉及是否向境内公众提供相关服务，第十七条另涉及舆论属性或社会动员能力。应据具体服务与原文判断；注册地、可直连或名单未匹配都不能单独裁定。
  18. 自建评测、接口差异、提示词重测、许可与数据条款、回滚方案（再加线上指标收尾）。
  19. 席位/固定成本、显存与电费、人工复核与返工；视频任务再加秒数与尝试次数。
  20. 引用回原文、第二人复核、边界与拒答题、记录失败与改法。
  1. Source preparation/indexing, retrieval, then grounded generation and citation checks. Reranking is optional, not mandatory in every RAG pipeline.
  2. If the needed knowledge is absent from documents, live system queries are required, permissions are unusually complex, or the task only needs reasoning or format conversion, RAG can add unnecessary components and failure points.
  3. Large chunks can mix noise and topics, weaken precise retrieval and consume context; small chunks can lose context, references and complete answers.
  4. Names, IDs and error codes may require exact matching that keyword retrieval can complement when vector retrieval misses them.
  5. Reranking reorders retrieved candidates to improve top-K relevance, at the cost of extra computation, calls and latency.
  6. Language coverage, cost and throughput, and evaluation on your own domain data.
  7. Information in the middle may be missed; long inputs can cost more; filling a window does not establish understanding, and retrieval quality still matters.
  8. Loss depends on the task and precision. Four-bit quantisation may be usable on many tasks but needs evaluation; quality does not fall by a fixed half.
  9. No. Inspect smaller models, lower precision, CPU/RAM offload or multi-device options. Check backend support, capacity, latency, communication and task quality separately; offload alone does not imply lower weight precision.
  10. Commercial-use scope and redistribution/derivative conditions; an open-source label alone does not establish the applicable rights.
  11. Training data/intended use, evaluation methods/results, known limitations, and version/date.
  12. Possible evaluation-data contamination, different prompts or task distributions, and inconsistent vendor-reported methods.
  13. Treat external content as untrusted data, separate instructions from sources, and apply least privilege plus output validation.
  14. Least privilege, allowlists, approval for consequential actions and revocable permissions.
  15. Sensitive material in prompts, unauthorised retrieval, logs/exports and tool calls sending data outward.
  16. Record region, role, intended use and risk assessment, then check obligations, legal version and applicability dates. Use the Commission's official references; do not treat an old timetable as current law.
  17. Article 2 concerns relevant services offered to the mainland public; Article 17 separately concerns public-opinion attributes or social-mobilisation capacity. Assess the actual service and text. Entity location, connectivity or a missing list match alone cannot decide applicability.
  18. Task evaluation, interface differences, prompt rechecks, licence/data terms, and rollback, followed by monitoring after rollout.
  19. Seats/fixed costs, hardware/power, and review/rework. Video also needs billed duration and attempt counts.
  20. Trace citations to original text, independently review, use boundary/abstention cases, and record failures and fixes.

评分标准Scoring

得分判定下一步
36–40综合练习用 exercise-builder 的交付物检验理解
28–35合格回看错题对应页面，重做错题
20–27需补基础先读「怎么评测 RAG 的效果」「怎么验证 AI 的输出」
0–19基础缺口从 快速入门 开始，按当前问题补基础

简答题按要点给分：答对核心 2 分；答对一半 1 分；答错或空白 0 分。评分线是练习约定，不是通用能力认证。

Award two points for the core points, one for partial accuracy and zero for an incorrect or blank answer. Thresholds are exercise conventions, not a general certification of ability.

复习路线Review path

  • 基础概念：快速入门。
  • 评测与验证：RAG 评测、输出验证。
  • 选型与合规：决策者路线、国内义务、EU AI Act 自查。
  • 动手：按任务选择 问答练习、创作练习 或 决策练习，无需全部按顺序完成。
  • Fundamentals: Quick start.
  • Evaluation and verification: RAG evaluation and output verification.
  • Selection and compliance: decision-maker route, mainland obligations and EU AI Act self-check.
  • Practice: choose the cited-answer exercise, creator exercise or decision exercise for your task. Completing all three in order is not required.
