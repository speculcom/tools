图像与视频：一条内容该用哪条工具链Image and video pipelines

规划Planning

先分图像与视频两条链，再按输入模态、权利条款与工作流位置选；每个候选写明适用条件与替代路线。

Split image and video into two chains, then choose by input modality, rights terms and workflow position; each candidate gets its conditions and an alternative.

Midjourney：适合重风格、接受社区公开形态的创作者。什么情况下选另一个：作品不能公开，选 FLUX 自托管或 Ideogram 商用许可线。

FLUX：适合要托管与自托管双路线、或要把模型放进端侧流水线的人。什么情况下选另一个：主要做海报文字排版，Ideogram 更对口。

Ideogram：适合图内文字、商品图与 precise edit。什么情况下选另一个：只要风格探索与社区参考，可对照 Midjourney 的工作流。

Midjourney: a candidate for creators who want style exploration and accept community publication. If work cannot be public, examine the actual privacy configuration of a self-hosted or separately licensed option such as FLUX or Ideogram.

FLUX: a candidate for hosted/self-hosted or on-device pipelines. For poster typography, compare Ideogram's workflow.

Ideogram: a candidate for text inside images, product imagery and precise edits. For style exploration and community reference, compare Midjourney's workflow.

Kling：适合国产、要长单段与多参考素材的团队。什么情况下选另一个：要 SynthID 溯源与 Google 生态，选 Veo。

Veo：适合要 API 交付、溯源标记与 Google 云集成的团队。什么情况下选另一个：主要在国内交付、要直连，选 Kling。

Runway：适合要平台化工作流、MCP / 插件与团队管控的人。什么情况下选另一个：只生成单条短片、不要平台绑定，选 Kling 或 Veo。

Kling: a candidate for domestic workflows needing longer individual shots and multiple reference assets. Examine Veo for SynthID and Google integration.

Veo: a candidate for API delivery, provenance markers and Google Cloud integration. Examine Kling if direct domestic access is a requirement.

Runway: a candidate for a platform workflow, MCP/plugins and team controls. Examine Kling or Veo when the task is a single short video and platform dependencies are unnecessary.

核验事实Dated facts

  1. Kling 4.0 单次原生 30 秒、全能参考支持 15 项素材（官方站，2026-10 档案核验）。
  2. Veo 输出带 SynthID 溯源；免费配额数据用于训练、付费不训练（官方条款，2026-10 档案核验）。
  3. Ideogram 4.0 开放量化权重，非商用免费、商用自托管需许可；4.5 为闭源托管（官方站，2026-10 档案核验）。
  4. FLUX 条款允许商用，但禁止用输出训练其他模型（官方条款，2026-10 档案核验）。
  5. Runway 的训练许可不可撤销、无退出开关（官方条款，2026-10 档案核验）。

以上日期为本站档案核验快照日；产品版本与配额以官方页面当日为准。

  1. The Kling 4.0 snapshot records native 30-second generation and up to 15 reference assets (official site; October 2026 dossier check).
  2. The Veo snapshot records SynthID and a free/paid data-training distinction under the cited service terms (official terms; October 2026 dossier check).
  3. The Ideogram snapshot records quantised 4.0 weights free for non-commercial use, separate commercial self-hosting licensing, and closed hosted 4.5 (official site; October 2026 dossier check).

一条内容链的顺序Pipeline order

  1. 定用途与权利：商用 / 非商用、是否可公开、是否需要溯源。
  2. 定输入模态：纯文字、参考图、首尾帧、还是多素材参考。
  3. 定工具位：图像定主视觉，视频定分镜；不要把图像模型硬拉去做 30 秒叙事。
  4. 定交付：平台内下载、API、还是自托管流水线。
  5. 留证据：保存生成参数、模型版本、许可页面截图与日期。
  1. Purpose and rights: commercial/non-commercial, publication requirements and provenance.
  2. Input modality: text, reference image, first/last frames or multiple assets.
  3. Workflow position: images for key visuals and video for shots; an image model is not a 30-second narrative pipeline by itself.
  4. Delivery: platform download, API or self-hosted pipeline.
  5. Evidence record: generation settings, model version, licence-page snapshot and date.

常见误判Common misreads

  • 把「付费」当成「商用许可」：订阅只解决使用门槛，商用还要看条款分层。
  • 把「开放权重」当成「随便商用」：Ideogram 4.0 的非商用免费与商用许可是两档。
  • 把「带水印」当成「不能用」：SynthID 是溯源标记，不等于版权归属。
  • 把「平台聚合」当成「自研模型」：Runway 同时聚合第三方模型，条款按模型分线。
  • Paying a subscription does not by itself establish every commercial right; check the applicable terms and tier.
  • Open weights do not imply unrestricted commercial use; the recorded Ideogram 4.0 non-commercial and commercial licences differ.
  • SynthID is a provenance marker, not a statement of copyright ownership.
  • A platform can aggregate other vendors' models; check terms for the selected model rather than assuming everything is first-party.
