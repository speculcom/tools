练习包 · Builder：用 10 篇站内文档搭一个带引用的问答Exercise: cited answers from 10 source pages

规划Planning

一个可独立完成的 RAG 练习：选 10 篇站内页面做语料，产出 10 题答案、引用表、评测表与失败记录；附评分标准与参考答案结构。

A self-contained RAG exercise: use 10 site pages as a corpus and produce 10 answers, a citation table, an evaluation sheet and a failure log, with a rubric and a model-answer outline.

任务The task

用 10 篇站内页面做唯一语料，搭一个能回答问题的问答流程。不允许接外部搜索，不允许凭记忆补充。题数和评分线是本练习的教学约束，不是生产系统的通用标准。

  1. 从 learn 站任选 10 个词条页，主题集中在一个领域（如 RAG 或本地部署）。
  2. 把它们切成可检索的片段，记录切分规则（按段、按标题、固定长度）。
  3. 写 10 个问题，其中 3 个的答案不在语料里，用来测拒答。
  4. 每次回答必须带引用：页面标题 + 链接 + 命中的原文句子。
  5. 记录每次失败：答错、答偏、编造、引用错页。

Use ten site pages as the only corpus for a question-answering workflow. Do not add external search or fill gaps from memory. The question count and passing score are teaching constraints for this exercise, not universal production standards.

  1. Choose ten Learn term pages concentrated in one area, such as RAG or local deployment.
  2. Split them into retrievable passages and record whether you split by paragraph, heading or fixed length.
  3. Write ten questions; three should have no answer in the corpus, to test abstention.
  4. Every answer must cite a page title, link and supporting source sentence.
  5. Record failures: incorrect answers, off-topic answers, fabrication or citations to the wrong page.

参考答案结构Model answer outline

不提供逐题答案（语料由你选），提供合格答案的结构：

问题：分块时为什么要保留标题和页码？
答案：它们可作为元数据，让检索时按结构信息过滤。
引用：《怎么给文档做分块》「怎么做」第 6 项；原文句：「保留结构信息：标题、章节、页码存进元数据，检索时能过滤。」
判定：对；原因：引用句直接支持答案。

拒答题的合格写法：

问题：这个模型在 2026-11-01 的准确率是多少？
答案：语料里没有该日期的评测数据，无法回答。需要补充官方评测页或自建评测。
判定：拒答；原因：语料中无对应事实。

The corpus is your choice, so this supplies an answer structure rather than answers to every question:

Question: Why preserve headings and page numbers when chunking?
Answer: They can be stored as metadata to filter retrieval by document structure.
Citation: How to Chunk Documents for Retrieval, “How to do it”, item 6.
Source sentence (Chinese original): 保留结构信息：标题、章节、页码存进元数据，检索时能过滤。
Verdict: correct; the cited sentence directly supports the answer.

An acceptable abstention:

Question: What was this model's accuracy on 2026-11-01?
Answer: The corpus contains no evaluation data for that date, so this cannot be answered.
An official evaluation page or a suitable evaluation would be needed.
Verdict: abstained; the required fact is absent from the corpus.

常见失分Common misses

  • 引用整页而不是句子：读者无法核对。
  • 把「语义相近」当「原文支持」：答案超出引用句。
  • 无答案题也答：宁可拒答，不要补记忆。
  • 只测正常输入会漏掉边界问题；补充无答案、缺字段和不符合格式的样本。
  • Citing a whole page instead of a supporting sentence makes checking difficult.
  • Semantic similarity does not establish support; an answer may go beyond the cited text.
  • Abstain on unanswerable questions rather than filling gaps from memory.
  • Normal inputs alone miss boundary cases. Add unanswerable questions, missing fields and malformed inputs.
