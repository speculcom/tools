权限与限制Permissions & limits

OAuth 3LO 用户鉴权：agent 可见面=所连账号在站点的权限（含项目级限制）； 写能力（建 issue/改页面）直接生效——按「它能替你改 wiki」设防。

OAuth 3LO user authorization: the agent-visible surface = the connected account's permissions on the site (including project-level restrictions); write capabilities (creating issues / editing pages) take effect directly — defend on the assumption "it can edit your wiki for you".
