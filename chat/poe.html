Poe is the "one account, many models" aggregator chat product (by Quora): GPT/Claude/DeepSeek/Veo etc. connect through Poe's side, users pick models directly, points-based billing, and a build-your-own-bot surface is on record; all data is relayed through Poe's servers.

适合与不适合When to use it, when not to

适合：横向对比多家模型、自建 bot 人设、含国产模型接入。 不适合：要免费无限量旗舰模型、要大陆直连、要数据不经第三方中转。

Fits: comparing many models side by side, building your own bots, domestic-model access included. Not a fit: unlimited free flagship models, direct mainland access, data not relayed through a third party.

头号误解Top misconception

  • Poe 是中转聚合层——你的对话经 Poe 服务器转发到各家模型 API，敏感数据要按「多一方经手」评估Poe is a relay/aggregation layer — your chats are forwarded from Poe's servers to each model API; assess sensitive data as "one more party handling it"
  • 点数制不是「免费无限」——旗舰模型与长上下文消耗快，免费档边界要实测The points system is not "free unlimited" — flagship models and long contexts burn points fast; the free-tier boundary needs measuring
  • 模型名带后缀（如 Claude-Sonnet-5）是 Poe 侧接入的具体版本——别把它等同于该厂最新能力面Model names with suffixes (e.g. Claude-Sonnet-5) are the specific versions Poe connects on its side — do not equate them with that vendor's latest capability
