讯飞星火是语音底座突出的国产对话产品（讯飞自研星火系列，语音识别/合成/同传是传统强项）： 教育/办公场景友好，首批备案（2023-08-23）；星火主线不开源权重。

iFlytek Spark is the domestic chat product with a strong voice foundation (iFlytek's self-developed Spark series; recognition/synthesis/interpretation are traditional strengths): education/office friendly, first-batch filed (2023-08-23); the Spark line does not open its weights.

适合与不适合When to use it, when not to

适合：语音交互密集、中文问答解题、教育办公文档。 不适合：要本机跑模型、要脱离讯飞账号。

Fits: voice-heavy interaction, Chinese Q&A and problem-solving tutoring, education/office documents. Not a fit: running models locally (Spark weights closed), leaving the iFlytek account.
