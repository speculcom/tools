权限与限制Permissions & limits

官方数据使用与共享说明：在「经安全加密技术处理、严格去标识化且无法重新识别特定个人」前提下， 输入、指令与生成回复可能用于模型训练；退出须联系客服登记（验证身份后 5-7 个工作日）， 账号级统一退出、不支持按对话退出，退出后历史与新增数据都不再进训练集。 Kimi Business 企业版承诺企业数据不进入模型训练流程，个人与企业空间完全隔离。

Official data usage & sharing notice: on the premise of "processing with secure encryption, strict de-identification such that specific individuals cannot be re-identified", inputs, prompts and generated replies may be used for model training; opting out requires registering with customer service (5-7 business days after identity verification), it is an account-level uniform opt-out with no per-chat opt-out, and after opting out neither historical nor new data enters the training set. Kimi Business promises enterprise data never enters the model training pipeline, with personal and enterprise spaces fully isolated.
